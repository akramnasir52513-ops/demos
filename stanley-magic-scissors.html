Style guide

Getting the Most From Your Cut

A few honest pointers from the chair — how to choose your fade, keep your beard in shape, and make your haircut last longer between visits.

Choosing Your Fade

Low fade — subtle and classic, the blend sits just above the ears. A safe choice for work and the easiest to grow out. Mid fade — the all-rounder, sharper than a low fade but still versatile. High fade — bold and striking, with the blend taken high for maximum contrast with the top.

Your head shape, hair type and how often you want to maintain it all matter. Tell us your routine and we will steer you to the fade that fits your life, not just the mirror.

Beard Care Between Visits

A beard looks its best with a little attention at home: brush it daily to train the growth, keep the neckline honest, and do not let the cheeks creep up. A drop of beard oil keeps things soft and stops the itch.

Leave the reshaping to us — a professional trim every few weeks restores the lines and takes off the straggly ends that make even a good beard look tired.

Making Your Cut Last

Most cuts look their sharpest for the first two to three weeks. Fades generally want a refresh sooner than longer styles. Book your next appointment before you leave the chair, or message us on WhatsApp when the edges start to soften — a quick tidy-up keeps you looking sharp without a full restyle.

Using the right product matters too: a small amount, warmed in your palms and worked through dry or nearly-dry hair, beats a big blob slapped on wet hair every time.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I book an appointment?

The quickest way is WhatsApp — message us on 07515 121692 and we will agree a time with you. You can also call 07765 180865 or message us through Facebook.

How much does a haircut cost?

Prices depend on the service, so we do not list them here — call or message us and we will tell you exactly what your cut, fade or shave costs before you book. No surprises, ever.

What services do you offer?

Haircuts, skin fades, beard trims and shaping, traditional hot towel shaves, kids cuts, and a full-works package that combines them. If you have something specific in mind, just ask.

Do you cut children's hair?

Yes — kids are very welcome, including first haircuts. We keep things calm and patient. If a quieter appointment would help, message us on WhatsApp and we will do our best.

What is a hot towel shave?

It is the traditional barber's shave: hot towels soften your skin and hair first, then a close, careful shave, finished with a cool towel. It gives the closest finish and is a proper bit of downtime too.

Can I show you a photo of the style I want?

Absolutely — photos help a lot. Bring one along or send it to us on WhatsApp beforehand, and we will talk honestly about how it will work with your hair.

How long does an appointment take?

It depends on the service — a tidy-up is quicker than the full works. When you book, tell us what you would like and we will let you know how long to allow.

What if I need to change my appointment?

Just message us on WhatsApp or give us a call as early as you can, and we will do our best to find you another time.

Where are you based?

We are in Stanley, County Durham. Message us on WhatsApp if you need directions or want to check anything before you visit.

Why are you called Magic Scissors?

Because that is what a great haircut feels like — you sit down one way and stand up transformed. No tricks though: just skilled hands, sharp tools and attention to detail.

How often should I get my fade redone?

As a general guide, skin fades look best refreshed every two to three weeks, while longer styles can go a little longer. When you notice the edges softening, message us on WhatsApp and we will fit you in for a tidy-up.

Can I book just for a beard trim?

Yes — beard trims and shaping are available on their own, as is a hot towel shave. Many customers combine them with a cut for the full works, but there is no obligation to.

Do you take group bookings, like a wedding party?

We do our best for groups — grooms and wedding parties especially. Message us on WhatsApp with the date and how many people, and we will plan the appointments with you.
