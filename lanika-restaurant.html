The Buffet

Fill your plate, go back for more — that's the Lanika way.

Our aim is simple: beautiful quality food in a chic, relaxed setting. The buffet lines up around half a dozen freshly cooked curries at any time — from mild and creamy to a proper spicy kick — alongside starters, sides and desserts, all prepared on site by our chefs using the finest, freshest ingredients.

Prefer to order from the menu? The main restaurant menu runs alongside the buffet, and everything is cooked to your taste. Families are well looked after too — there's even chips and nuggets for the fussier little ones.
