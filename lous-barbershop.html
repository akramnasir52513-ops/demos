About Us

A proper family barbershop

Lou's Barbershop is a multi-award-winning family barbershop in Quedgeley, established in 2014 and co-owned by Rob and Lou. Lou brings nearly 15 years of barbering experience and Rob over 8 — together with their team of qualified barbers they look after gents and kids of all ages in a friendly, relaxed shop.

Alongside everyday cuts and fades, the shop offers traditional hot towel services and a non-surgical hair replacement service, creating custom hairpieces that blend seamlessly with your natural hair. Appointments and walk-ins are both welcome.
