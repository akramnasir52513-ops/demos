The shop

Old-school craft, no fuss

The Gentry is a traditional barber's on Kirkgate, in the heart of Leeds. Three chairs, proper tools, and barbers who take the time to get it right — whether that's a seamless skin fade or a straight-razor shave your grandad would approve of.

No rushed conveyor belt, no £40 haircuts. Just a good cut, a fair price, and a bit of crack in the chair.

  • Hot-towel finish on every shave
  • Free fringe & neck tidy between cuts
  • Kids welcome — patient with first cuts
