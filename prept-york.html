About Prept York

Prept York is an award-winning salon in the heart of York, offering a wide range of beauty treatments from brows and lashes to nails, spray tanning and facials. The salon is bright and fresh, yet cosy and relaxing — the perfect retreat to escape from it all.

Find us at 62 Goodramgate, York YO1 7LF. The experienced team is fully qualified and uses the latest equipment and products, with a down-to-earth, inclusive welcome for everyone.
