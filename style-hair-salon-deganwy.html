Good To Know

Your first visit, and caring for your hair at home

New to Style? Here's what to expect when you walk through the door — plus the simple, honest hair-care advice we give our own clients.

What happens at your appointment

Every appointment at Style starts the same way: with a proper chat. We'll ask what you like about your hair, what frustrates you, and what you'd like to change. If you're going for a restyle or a new colour, feel free to bring photos — they help us understand exactly the look you have in mind.

  • A friendly consultation before any scissors come out
  • Clear explanation of what we'll do and what it will cost
  • A relaxing wash and treatment at the basin where needed
  • Styling and finishing included, so you leave ready for anything
  • Aftercare advice tailored to your new style

Running late or need to rearrange? Life happens — just send us a WhatsApp message as soon as you can and we'll do our best to find you another time.

🌿 Between colour appointments

Coloured hair stays vibrant longer when you wash it a little less often, use lukewarm rather than very hot water, and choose shampoo made for colour-treated hair. If your roots are showing through before your next visit, message us — a regrowth appointment or a toner refresh can bridge the gap beautifully.

🔥 Heat styling, the kind way

Always use a heat-protection spray before straighteners, tongs or a hot brush, and keep the temperature as low as your hair type allows. One slow pass usually beats three fast ones — and your ends will thank you for it. A weekly deep-conditioning treatment makes a real difference to dry or highlighted hair.

✂️ How often should you book?

Short styles and gents cuts usually look sharpest with a trim every 4–6 weeks. Longer hair can often go 8–12 weeks between cuts, while colour clients typically rebook every 6–8 weeks for regrowth. Not sure? Ask us at your appointment and we'll suggest a rhythm that suits your hair and your budget.

Do I need a skin test before colour?

For many colour services, yes — a quick skin (patch) test is standard professional practice and helps keep you safe from allergic reactions. When you book a colour appointment, we'll let you know exactly what's needed and when to pop in for it.

What's the difference between balayage and highlights?

Highlights are usually created with foils for an even, brighter result from root to tip. Balayage is hand-painted for a softer, more natural, sun-kissed effect with a gentler grow-out. Not sure which is right for you? Message us a photo of your hair and the look you'd like, and we'll advise honestly.

Do you cut men's hair too?

Yes — we offer gents cuts alongside our ladies' services, from classic styles to modern fades and tidy-ups. The whole family is welcome at Style.

Can you style hair for weddings and special occasions?

Absolutely. We offer occasion styling including hair-up, curls and polished finishes for weddings, proms, parties and photos. We recommend booking occasion hair in good time — message us with your date and we'll plan it with you.

Style Guide

Popular looks, explained honestly

Not sure what to ask for? Here's a plain-English guide to the styles clients ask us about most — what they involve, who they suit, and how much upkeep to expect. When you're ready, message us a photo of your hair and the look you like, and we'll tell you honestly what's achievable.

The lived-in balayage

Balayage remains the most requested colour in salons like ours, and for good reason: the colour is painted on freehand, brighter through the ends and softer at the roots, so it grows out gracefully with no harsh line. It suits almost everyone, works on brunettes as beautifully as on blondes, and is the ideal choice if you want to stretch the time between colour appointments. Maintenance is refreshingly low — many clients return every three to four months for a top-up, with a toner in between if they want to keep the shade crisp. If your hair is very dark and you dream of icy blonde ends, we'll talk you through the journey honestly: getting there safely can take more than one appointment, and we'd always rather protect your hair's condition than rush it.

The glossy full colour

There's nothing quite like the shine and richness of a fresh full-head colour. Whether you're covering greys, deepening your natural shade or making a proper change, a full colour gives an even, polished result from root to tip. Grey coverage is one of the most rewarding services we do — done well, it takes years off and blends seamlessly so nobody would ever guess. Expect to rebook every six to eight weeks for regrowth, depending on how fast your hair grows and how much contrast there is between your natural colour and your chosen shade. We'll always check your hair's condition first and recommend a treatment if your ends need some love before we colour.

Occasion hair that lasts all day

Weddings, proms, parties and race days all call for hair that looks incredible at the first photograph and still looks incredible at the last dance. Our occasion styling ranges from soft romantic waves and classic chignons to modern textured up-dos. Our honest advice: book as early as you can for wedding parties, and if you're the bride, a trial appointment is worth every penny — it lets us test the style, the timings and how your hair holds, so the big day itself is calm and stress-free. Bring along photos of your dress and any accessories or veils you plan to wear; the more we know, the better we can make the hair work with the whole look.

Can I bring my children for a haircut?

Yes — younger clients are very welcome at Style. A calm, friendly village salon is a great place for a child's first proper haircut, and we'll always take things at their pace. Just mention the children's cuts when you message us to book, so we can allow the right amount of time.

What if I need to cancel or rearrange?

We understand that plans change. If you can't make your appointment, please let us know on WhatsApp as early as you can — that gives us the chance to offer the slot to someone else, and we'll happily help you find a new time that suits you better.

My hair is damaged — can you still colour it?

Often, yes — but the honest answer is "it depends". When you get in touch, tell us about your hair's history (previous colour, bleaching, heat damage) and we'll advise you properly. Sometimes a treatment plan and a trim come first, and the colour follows once your hair is stronger. We'd always rather be straight with you than risk your hair.

Do you sell gift vouchers?

Pop us a message on WhatsApp or ask in the salon and we'll let you know what we can arrange. A salon appointment makes a lovely gift for birthdays, Mother's Day and Christmas — and it's a great way to introduce someone new to Style.
