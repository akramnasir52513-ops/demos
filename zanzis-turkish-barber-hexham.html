Look after it

Keeping it sharp between visits

A great cut deserves a little care at home. Our barbers' simple advice for the days after your appointment:

📅

Rebook fades regularly

Skin fades look their sharpest in the first couple of weeks. Regular tidy-up appointments keep the blend clean and the lines crisp — message us any time to book your next one.

🧴

A little product goes far

Use a small amount of styling product on dry hair and work it through with your fingers. Less is more — you can always add, and it keeps the finish natural rather than heavy.

🪮

Brush the beard down

A quick brush through your beard each morning trains it to sit the way it was sculpted in the chair, and a drop of beard oil keeps it soft and the skin underneath comfortable.

🚿

Mind the hot water

Very hot water dries out skin and hair, especially after a close shave. Warm water and a gentle moisturiser will keep that fresh-from-the-chair feeling going longer.

The Zanzi standard

Small details, done every single time

A Turkish barber appointment is a ritual of small details, and it is the details that separate a good cut from a great one. At Zanzi's, every appointment follows the same standard — whether it is your first visit or your fiftieth.

It starts with listening. Before a single hair is cut, we talk through what you want, how your hair grows, and how you style it at home. The best barbering is a conversation, not a conveyor belt — that is how you end up with a cut that still looks right two weeks later, not just two minutes later.

Then comes the craft. Clippers and scissors in practiced hands, blends checked from every angle and in proper light, lines edged cleanly with the cut-throat razor. We would rather take an extra minute than let you leave with a line we would not wear ourselves.

And it ends with the finish. A proper tidy around the ears and neck, stray hairs dealt with, styling shown — not just done — so you can recreate the look at home, and honest advice on when to come back to keep it sharp.

That standard is why customers travel in from across the Tyne Valley and leave us the reviews they do. Whether you have been coming for years or it is your very first visit, the chair, the ritual and the care are exactly the same.
