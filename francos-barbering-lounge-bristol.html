About us

Franco's Barbering Lounge has been helping men look and feel their best on Gloucester Road, Bristol, for nearly 40 years. Traditional barbering skill combined with modern men's styling, personal consultations with every appointment, and a relaxed, professional shop.

Services

Traditional Barbering

Classic, timeless cuts from a team barbering on Gloucester Road for nearly 40 years.

Modern Styling & Fades

Current styles, sharp fades and textured finishes, shaped to your face and hair type.

Beard Grooming

Beard shaping, trims and hot-towel finishes for a clean, finished look.

Colour, Grey Blending & Highlights

Grey blending, colour and highlights, plus wedding hair consultations and hair-loss advice.

Questions, answered

Do I need to book, or can I walk in?

Booking ahead is best — email us with your preferred day and time and we will confirm your slot.

How do I book?

Email us at the address on this page with the service you want. We reply to confirm.

Do you cut children's hair?

Yes — family appointments are welcome. Tell us the age when you book so we allow the right time.

What should I do if I need to cancel?

Email us as early as you can so we can offer your slot to someone else.
