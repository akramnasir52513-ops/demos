About Jodie

Your Skin, Truly Understood

A solo specialist practice — no rushed conveyor belt, no one-size-fits-all facials.

“Your skin tells a story — stress, time, and life can dull its glow, but the right plan can restore it. At my private home salon, I specialise in personalised, results-driven skin treatments designed to bring your skin back to its healthiest, most confident state.”

Using advanced technologies such as Nimue Skin Technology, SkinPen® Precision microneedling, Dermalux LED light therapy and Calecim Professional, every treatment is carefully tailored to your unique skin concerns — whether that’s ageing, pigmentation, acne, sensitivity, or loss of radiance.

This is a calm, professional space where your skin is truly understood, your results are prioritised, and you can relax knowing you’re in expert hands.

Jodie specialises in:

Ageing & fine lines Pigmentation Acne & congestion Sensitive skin Loss of radiance Acne scarring Skin texture

New to advanced skincare? Every skin journey starts with a complimentary prescriptive consultation — a professional skin analysis and a personalised plan before you book a single treatment.
