Your Visit, Step by Step

First time with us? Here's exactly what happens.

1. The Chat

Every appointment starts with a quick conversation — what you're after, what will suit your hair and how it grows. Bring a photo of the style you want if you like; it always helps.

2. The Cut

Clippers and scissors do the work in stages: bulk off first, then the blend, then the detail. We check the shape as we go, so there are no surprises at the end.

3. The Finish

Edges sharpened, neckline tidied, a hot towel where it counts, and the style put in so you can see the finished look. A final mirror check — front and back — before you go.
