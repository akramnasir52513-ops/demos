Our Services

Classic barbering done properly — every service starts with a consultation so you get exactly the look you came in for.

Haircuts

From timeless classics to modern textured styles, your haircut begins with a proper conversation about what you want and what suits your hair and face shape. Your barber works with precision clippers and scissors, checks every angle in the mirror with you, and finishes with styling so you leave knowing how to recreate the look at home.

  • Consultation before the first cut
  • Scissor and clipper work tailored to you
  • Styled finish and home-care advice

Skin Fades

A skin fade lives or dies on the blend, and that is where we take our time. Whether you want a low, mid or high fade, your barber builds the gradient gradually for a seamless transition with no harsh lines. The edges are sharpened and tidied for that crisp, clean finish a great fade is known for.

  • Low, mid and high fades available
  • Seamless, gradual blending — no lines
  • Sharp, tidy edges and neckline
Email for prices — just ask when you book

Beard Trim & Hot Towel Shave

Proper beard care is about shape, symmetry and healthy hair. We trim and sculpt your beard to suit your face, tidy the lines with a cut-throat razor, and finish with oils to keep it soft and conditioned. Add a traditional hot towel shave for the closest, smoothest finish — an old-school ritual done the right way.

  • Beard sculpted to your face shape
  • Hot towels to soften and open pores
  • Razor-sharp lines and conditioning finish

The Full Works

The complete Crown experience in one appointment: a full consultation, your chosen haircut or fade, a beard trim or hot towel shave, and a styled finish. Questions & Answers

Everything you might want to know before you book.

How do I book an appointment?

Booking is by email — just send a message to crownbarbers.inverness@gmail.com telling us which service you would like and when suits you, and we will confirm your appointment.

How much does a haircut cost?

Prices depend on the service, so please message for prices when you email — we will give you a clear price for exactly what you need before you book.

Where are you located?

You will find us in Inverness. Email us at crownbarbers.inverness@gmail.com for directions and we will point you straight to the shop door.

Do I need to book, or can I walk in?

The shop has a walk-in friendly atmosphere, but booking ahead by email is the best way to guarantee your slot — especially at weekends and other busy times.

I am not sure what style to ask for — can you help?

Absolutely. Every appointment starts with a consultation, so just come as you are — show us a photo if you have one, or describe what you like, and your barber will advise what will suit your hair and face shape.

Do you offer beard trims and shaves?

Yes — beard trims and traditional hot towel shaves are a speciality here. You can book them on their own or together with a cut as part of The Full Works.
