Looking After Your Results

Simple aftercare tips

A little care goes a long way. These general tips help most clients get the best from their treatments.

After brow lamination

Keep your brows dry and avoid steam, saunas and heavy workouts for the first 24 hours. After that, brush them into place daily and condition them regularly to keep the hairs soft and healthy.

After a lash lift

Keep lashes dry for the first 24 hours and avoid rubbing your eyes. Sleep on your back if you can on the first night, and skip waterproof mascara, which needs harsher removal.

Between appointments

Resist over-plucking between visits — leaving regrowth alone gives us more to work with at your next shape. Tint and lift results fade gradually, so booking your next appointment in good time keeps the look continuous.

Compare Treatments

Brows & lashes at a glance

A quick guide to what each treatment does — and who it suits best.

For tidy, natural brows

Choose brow shaping, with a brow tint if your hairs are fair or sparse-looking. Together they clean up the shape and add the colour and depth that make brows look naturally fuller — the ideal regular maintenance pairing.

For full, fluffy brows

Choose brow lamination. It's the treatment to pick if your brows are unruly, grow in different directions, have gaps, or simply won't hold a brushed-up style with gel alone. Add a tint for maximum definition.

For maximum definition

Choose HD brows for brows and a lash lift with tint for lashes. This is the full before-an-event combination — brows designed, coloured and styled, with lashes lifted and darkened for a complete, polished look.

Do I need a patch test?

A patch test is usually needed before tinting and lamination treatments, especially if it's your first time or you haven't had the treatment for a while. When you book, we'll let you know if you need one and arrange it with you.

What's the difference between brow shaping, lamination and HD brows?

Brow shaping tidies and sculpts your natural brows. Lamination re-sets the hairs in a lifted direction for a fuller, fluffier look. HD brows is a bespoke multi-step treatment combining custom tinting, shaping and styling for the most defined finish.

How long does brow lamination last?

Results typically last around 4–6 weeks, depending on your natural hair growth cycle and how well the aftercare advice is followed. Keeping brows conditioned helps them stay looking their best.

How long does a lash lift last?

A lash lift typically lasts around 6–8 weeks as your natural lashes grow and shed. Adding a lash tint at the same appointment gives extra definition for the whole of that period.

Can I wear makeup after my treatment?

It's best to keep the treated area clean and dry for the first 24 hours after lamination, tinting and lash lifts. After that, you can return to your normal makeup routine — many clients find they need far less.

I'm not sure which treatment I need — can you advise?
