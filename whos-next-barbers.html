The shop

Barbering Lockerbie keeps recommending

Who's Next is home to highly skilled, professional barbers who are attentive, friendly and take real pride in every cut — the kind of shop where your beard gets tamed and styled to look better than when you walked in.

The chair everyone talks about belongs to Gary Lewis — a Men's Stylist of the Year finalist at the Scottish Hair & Beauty Awards in both 2019 and 2021. One fellow barber's review credits his 18 years of experience across barbering and hairdressing — and says he's never seen such passion or cutting style.

Busy? Last-minute appointments and walk-ins are available, there are weekend appointments on Saturdays, and the shop has wheelchair-accessible parking.

In the chair

Services at Who's Next

Excellent hairstyles at an affordable price — here's what's on offer at 87 High Street.

01

Haircuts

Precision cuts tailored to you — from classic short back and sides to modern styles and sharp fades, cut, finished and styled by barbers who take great pride in their work.

02

Beard Trims

Your burly friend, tamed. Beards trimmed, shaped and styled to look better than ever — the perfect finish to a fresh cut, or a tidy-up on its own.

03

Kids' Cuts

We're known for being great with children. Nervous first-timers get a calm, patient, unhurried cut — parents tell us breakthrough haircuts happen in this chair, sometimes in and out in 20 minutes with a smile.

04

Fades & Creative Designs

Skin fades and creative clipper designs — like this one, cut right here in the shop. Bring us a picture, or let Gary freestyle something of your own.
