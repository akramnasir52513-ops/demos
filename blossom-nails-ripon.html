Welcome

A Fresh New Name for Ripon Nails

You may know us as Royal Nails Beauty and Spa — we rebranded as The Blossom in May 2026.

The Blossom Nails & Beauty Spa sits on Westgate in Ripon, offering everything from a classic French finish to full 3D floral nail art. The salon is open seven days a week and happily welcomes walk-ins as well as bookings.

Whether it’s everyday-perfect BIAB, a chrome finish for a night out, or bridal nails for the big day, the team will help you choose a look that suits your hands and your plans.

To book, send us a WhatsApp message — it’s the quickest way to reach the salon.
