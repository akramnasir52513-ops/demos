Welcome to Celestial

Warm, relaxing — and loved by regulars

Celestial Beauty is a warm and relaxing salon on Stafford Street, just a short walk from Walsall train station and easy to reach by bus or car. Step in for a revitalising facial, a lash lift and tint, or glam up your nails with a Shellac manicure.

Our expert therapists work in a calm, tranquil atmosphere — buff, polish and exfoliate, and leave feeling like a brand new person. As one regular puts it, Sarb and her team are “always willing to go that extra mile”.

Beauty, waxing, massage & laser — whatever your favourite, we’ve got you covered.
