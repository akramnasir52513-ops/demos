About The Godbarbers

The Godbarbers is a gentleman’s barbershop on Farmstead Road in Corby, offering skin fades, classic cuts and beard trims in a warm and friendly environment. Shop owner Lauren and the team take the time to get every cut right, so you leave looking sharp and feeling looked after.
