About J'adore Nails & Beauty

J'adore Nails & Beauty's technicians use the latest techniques and products, and are well-trained and fully certified. The salon offers BIAB builder gel, acrylic extensions, gel polish, nail art, brows, lashes, facials and waxing, using OPI, CND, Shellac, Dermalogica and Sienna X.

Find us at 34 Blandford Street, Sunderland SR1 3JJ. Relax in the modern pedicure spa chair, and ask about discounts, the loyalty scheme and bespoke packages.
