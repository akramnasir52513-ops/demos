Choosing your perfect brow

Temporary or semi-permanent, subtle or statement — here is how the most popular treatments at the clinic compare.

Microblading & powder brows — the semi-permanent options

If you are tired of drawing your brows on every morning, permanent makeup gives you a shape that stays. Microblading creates fine, individual hair strokes for the most natural finish, while powder and ombré brows heal to a soft, shaded effect — like a perfectly applied brow powder.

  • Shape designed and drawn on first — approved by you before we start
  • Pigment colour matched to your hair and skin tone
  • Aftercare guidance provided after every appointment
  • A top-up session perfects the healed result

Lamination & lash lift — the no-commitment glow-up

Prefer to keep things temporary? Brow lamination and lash lift work with what you already have. Lamination smooths unruly hairs into a fuller, uniform shape, and a lash lift curls your natural lashes upwards so your eyes look brighter without mascara or extensions.

  • Works with your natural brows and lashes
  • Perfect before holidays, weddings and events
  • Can be combined with a tint for extra definition
  • Simple aftercare — just keep them dry at first

Shape & tint — the classic maintenance appointment

Sometimes all great brows need is a professional shape and the right tint. Regular shaping keeps your natural brow line clean and balanced, while tinting picks up the fine, pale hairs you didn't know you had — making brows look instantly fuller.

  • Mapping to your bone structure, not a one-size stencil
  • Wax, thread and tweeze techniques for a clean finish
  • Tint shades mixed to suit your colouring

Frequently asked questions

Anything else you'd like to know? Just ask on WhatsApp — we're happy to help.

What's the difference between microblading and powder brows?

Microblading creates fine, individual strokes that mimic real brow hairs, giving the most natural, hair-like finish. Powder and ombré brows heal to a soft, shaded effect — similar to wearing brow powder — with a lighter front fading to a deeper tail. At your consultation we'll advise which suits your skin type and the look you want.

How long do semi-permanent brows last?

It varies from person to person depending on skin type, lifestyle and aftercare, but semi-permanent brows are designed to be long-lasting, with a top-up appointment used to perfect the healed result and refresh the colour over time. We'll explain what to expect honestly at your consultation.

Does microblading hurt?

Most clients describe it as a light scratching sensation rather than pain. The treatment is carried out carefully and at your pace — if you're nervous, mention it when you book and we'll talk you through exactly what to expect.

What is brow lamination?

Brow lamination is a treatment that relaxes your brow hairs and sets them in a brushed-up direction, making brows look fuller, fluffier and more uniform. It's a great option if your hairs grow downwards, are unruly, or your brows have gaps you'd like to disguise — no needles involved.

How long does a lash lift last?

A lash lift works with your natural lash cycle, so results gradually relax as your lashes naturally shed and regrow. Tinting keeps them looking dark and defined in the meantime. We'll advise on the best rebooking rhythm for you at your appointment.

Do I need a consultation before permanent makeup?

Yes — a consultation is an important part of any PMU treatment. It's where we discuss your medical history, assess your skin and brows, design your shape and choose pigment shades, so your treatment is planned properly and safely before it goes ahead.

What should I do before my appointment?

Arrive with your brows in their natural state where possible, and avoid heavy makeup on the brow area. For permanent makeup, follow any preparation advice given at your consultation carefully — it helps you get the best possible healed result.
