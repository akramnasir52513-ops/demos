Where is the salon?

We are in Inverness. Email us at lethaimo95@gmail.com for directions and we will be happy to help you find us.

How long do gel nails and extensions last?

Gel typically lasts 2–3 weeks with care. Extensions grow out with your natural nails and usually need infills every 2–3 weeks to keep them looking fresh and strong.

Do I need an appointment?

Booking ahead by email is recommended so we can reserve your time. Send us a message to check what is available this week.

Do you remove gel and acrylic nails?

Yes, we provide removal services. Proper removal keeps your natural nails healthy — never peel product off, just book a removal with us instead.
