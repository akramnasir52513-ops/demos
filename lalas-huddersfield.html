Welcome to Lala’s

Fine Indian Dining in a Landmark Setting

Set inside a grand former bank on St George’s Square — right opposite the station — Lala’s has been serving Huddersfield since 2013, earning a place among the town’s top-rated Indian restaurants.

The menu reflects the diversity of India: classic curries rooted in tradition with modern twists, traditional desi-style karahis, house baltis, charcoal tandoori sizzlers and fragrant biryanis — with a wide choice of vegetarian and non-vegetarian dishes, all made with fresh ingredients.

The house special balti — a mouth-watering mix of chicken, meat, mushrooms and prawns in fresh spices — is a must-try, and the family naans are famously generous. Dine in for celebrations and gatherings, or order a takeaway to enjoy at home.
