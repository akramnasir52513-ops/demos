Grooming Guide

Keeping It Sharp Between Visits

📅

How Often Should You Visit?

Skin fades look their best for around two weeks and stay tidy for about three. Classic scissor cuts can stretch to four or five weeks. Beards benefit from a professional reshape every two to three weeks.

🧴

Beard Care at Home

Wash your beard with warm water, pat it dry, and work in a little beard oil while it is still slightly damp. Brush it through daily — it trains the hairs to lie in the direction of your shaped style.

💈

Choosing Your Style

Bring a photo if you have one — it genuinely helps. Your barber will tell you honestly whether it suits your hair type and head shape, and suggest the closest version that will work for you.

🎉

Weddings & Big Occasions

Getting married, starting a new job, or have a big night coming up? Book the full works a day or two before — cut, hot towel shave and beard shaping — so you look sharp but natural in the photos.

⚽

How much does a haircut or shave cost?

Prices vary by service, so call or message us on WhatsApp for the current price of the service you want — we will confirm before you book, with no surprises.

What is a Turkish hot towel shave?

It is a traditional wet shave: hot towels prepare and soften the skin, a rich lather is applied, and a cut-throat razor gives an exceptionally close finish. A cool towel and soothing products complete the ritual.

What is ear singeing?

Ear singeing (also called ear flaming) is a traditional Turkish technique where a small, controlled flame is used to burn away fine hairs on the ears. It is quick, safe in trained hands, and leaves a clean finish that trimmers cannot match.

Do you do skin fades?

Yes — skin fades are a speciality. High, mid and low fades, all blended seamlessly and finished with razor-sharp edges.

Can I bring my child for a haircut?

Yes — combine a haircut or fade with a hot towel shave, beard shaping and ear singeing for the complete traditional package. Just ask when you book.

Do I need an appointment, or can I just walk in?

Both work. Walk-ins are always welcome, but at peak times — weekends, evenings and before big events — there can be a wait. Booking on WhatsApp takes under a minute and guarantees your slot.

How long does an appointment take?

A standard haircut or fade usually takes around half an hour, and a hot towel shave a similar time. The full works — cut, shave, beard shaping and finishing touches — takes longer, so allow extra time and enjoy it.

Is ear singeing safe?

Yes, in trained hands. It is a traditional technique barbers have used for generations — a small controlled flame passed quickly over the ear to burn away fine hairs. It does not touch the skin and is finished in seconds.

What should I ask for if I am not sure what suits me?

Just say so when you arrive. Your barber will look at your hair type, face shape and how you normally wear it, then recommend a style that suits you and is easy to maintain.

Can you fix a bad haircut from somewhere else?
