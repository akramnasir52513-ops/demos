About Cinderella Nails

Based at 84–86 Eastgate Street, Cinderella Nails is a nail spa in Gloucester city centre, listed by the Gloucester Chamber of Commerce among the city's hair & beauty businesses.

From a classic manicure to a full set of extensions finished with hand-painted art, the salon offers the full range of nail care — and you can book ahead through Fresha or simply call the salon to arrange your appointment.

Whether it's everyday polish or nails for a special occasion, pop in and let the team work their magic — no pumpkin required.
