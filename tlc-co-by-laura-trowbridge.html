About the salon

A salon Trowbridge has trusted since 2001

Tucked away on Sycamore Grove, TLC & Co by Laura has built its name the honest way — one happy client at a time. With a 4.8-star rating from 44 Google reviews, regulars praise the friendly team, the relaxed atmosphere and stylists who listen before they cut or colour.

It is a full hair and beauty salon: cuts and colour for all hair types, nails and lashes, plus Sienna X facials on Fridays and Saturdays, spray tanning and sugaring. One appointment can cover the whole pamper list.
