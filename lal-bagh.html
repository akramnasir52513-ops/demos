Welcome to Lal Bagh

Cooking With Passion, Served With Pride

Lal Bagh offers a unique, sophisticated dining experience for those who are passionate about their food. Every dish is prepared with fresh herbs and spices, and each has its own distinctive flavour and aroma — because it is essential to us that every customer feels at home.

Our building dates back to the early 14th century, in the heart of Weobley — a village renowned for its timber-framed houses. Behind the kitchen is our highly trained, award-winning chef Mr Salam, whose love of cooking began in his mother's kitchen and has been recognised in some of the finest restaurants.
