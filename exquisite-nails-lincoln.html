How long do gel nails last?

With attentive care, gel polish typically looks its finest for around two to three weeks before a refresh or infill is due. Daily cuticle oil, gloves for household tasks, and gentle use will preserve the finish beautifully.

Do I need an appointment, or can I walk in?

We recommend reserving by email so that your appointment is dedicated entirely to you. Please email first and we shall confirm availability.

Can you remove my old gel or acrylics?

Yes — professional, gentle removal is available and preserves the condition of your natural nails. Kindly mention your current set when booking so we may allow sufficient time.
