Questions & Answers

Everything you may wish to know, answered softly and clearly.

How do I book an appointment?

Booking is by email. Write to enrosenails20@gmail.com with the service you would love and when suits you, and we will reply to confirm your appointment.

How much do treatments cost?

Please message for prices — the cost depends on your service and design. Email us your idea and we will confirm the price before you book, with no surprises.

Where are you located?

We are in Dundee. For directions, please email us at enrosenails20@gmail.com and we will be delighted to guide you to us.

How long will my nails last?

Gel typically lasts 2–3 weeks with care, and extensions usually need gentle infills every 2–3 weeks as they grow. Treat your nails kindly and they will reward you.

Do I need an appointment?

We recommend booking by email so we can keep your visit calm and unhurried, with a time reserved just for you.

Do you offer removal services?

Yes, we remove gel and acrylic gently and safely. Please never peel product away yourself — book a removal and we will care for your natural nails properly.
