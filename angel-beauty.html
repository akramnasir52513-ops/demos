A Calm Retreat in the Heart of Cowley

Located in Templars Square in the heart of Oxford, Angel Beauty Parlour has been welcoming clients since 2011. Our small, diligent team offers a wide range of beauty services in a comfortable, relaxing and friendly atmosphere — with a personalised, professional service on every visit.

We are a women-only salon, and every guest is welcomed with complimentary refreshments, making each visit feel like a special occasion. Paid parking is available nearby.
