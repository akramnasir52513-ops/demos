About the shop

A Wibsey institution since 2010

Ramsons Barber Shop was founded in 2010 by Khuram Ramzan with one simple mission: to give men an unrivalled sense of confidence by knowing they look their best.

The team blends traditional barbering techniques with a modern twist — the kind of attention to detail that has made Ramsons an award-winning shop and set the standard other barbershops in the region follow. From traditional cuts to modern trends, every service is finished with premium men's grooming products.

No waiting around either — Ramsons runs an appointment system, so book ahead, walk in on time and sit straight down in the chair.

Khuram Ramzan — Founder, Ramsons Barber Shop
