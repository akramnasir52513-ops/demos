Questions & Answers

Helpful things to know before your visit.

How do I book an appointment?

Booking is by email. Send your enquiry to amynailsreading@gmail.com, tell us which service you would like and when suits you, and we will reply to confirm your appointment.

How much do treatments cost?

Message for prices — email us the service you are interested in and we will confirm the price with your booking, so everything is clear before you arrive.

Where is the studio?

We are in Reading. Email us at amynailsreading@gmail.com for directions and to confirm your appointment details before you visit.

How long do gel nails last?

With normal care, gel polish typically looks its best for around two to three weeks before a refresh is due. Daily cuticle oil, gloves for washing up, and treating your nails gently will help your set last its best.

Do I need an appointment, or can I walk in?

As a small studio, we work by appointment so your time is fully yours. Please email us first and we will confirm a time that suits you.

Can you remove my old gel or acrylics?

Yes — gentle, safe removal is available and is much kinder to your natural nails than picking product off. Just mention it when you email to book so we can allow enough time.
