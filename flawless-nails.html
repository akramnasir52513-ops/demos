About Us

Shaw's glam little gem

Flawless Nails & Beauty was established in 2015 and opened its Market Street salon in Shaw in 2016. Inside you'll find a modern, welcoming space offering all nail, beauty and waxing treatments — from everyday gel polish to full glam acrylics, LVL lashes, microblading and even crystal-clear microdermabrasion.

With a 5.0 rating and over 1,980 reviews on Fresha, our clients across Shaw, Royton and Oldham keep coming back for the friendly team and flawless finish. Book early — chairs fill fast!
