Our Services

Straightforward services, done to a high standard. Every cut starts with a quick consultation — tell us what you want, we will make it happen.

Skin Fades

The fade is our bread and butter. Low, mid or high, we build the blend in stages so the gradient is smooth all the way down to the skin — no steps, no patches, no harsh lines. Finished with crisp edges around the hairline and neck for a fade that looks fresh for longer.

  • Low, mid and high skin fades
  • Smooth, even blending throughout
  • Crisp edges and clean neckline

Beard Trim & Hot Towel Shave

A good beard needs shape, not just length taken off. We sculpt your beard to balance your face, define the cheek and neck lines with a razor, and condition the hair so it looks and feels healthier. The hot towel shave opens the pores first for the closest, smoothest shave possible.

  • Beard shaped to suit your face
  • Hot towel prep for a closer shave
  • Razor-defined lines and conditioned finish

Questions & Answers

The things people ask us most.

How do I book an appointment?

By email — send a message to barberroadmanagement@gmail.com with the service you want and a time that suits you, and we will confirm your booking.

How much do your services cost?

Please message for prices when you email to book — we will confirm the price for your exact service up front, so there are no surprises.

Where is the shop?

We are based in Edinburgh. Email us at barberroadmanagement@gmail.com for directions and we will help you find us easily.

Do I need to book, or can I just walk in?

We are walk-in friendly, but booking by email guarantees your chair — weekends especially can get busy, so it is worth messaging ahead.

I don’t know what style to ask for. What should I do?

Just ask. Bring a photo if you have one, or tell us what you like and dislike about your hair now — your barber will recommend a style that suits your hair type and face shape.

Do you do beard trims and shaves?

Yes — beard trims and hot towel shaves are available on their own, or combined with a cut in The Full Works package.
