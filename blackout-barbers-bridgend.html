About Blackout Barbers

Blackout Barbers is a local barber shop in the heart of Bridgend, built on a simple idea: a haircut should never feel rushed. When you sit in one of our chairs, you get a barber who listens first — to what you want, what your hair actually does, and what will work for your day-to-day life — and only then picks up the clippers.

Our barbers take real pride in the details: clean blends, tidy edges and a finish you can keep looking good at home without a shelf full of products. Whether you're in for a regular tidy-up, a complete restyle or the full works with beard and hot towel, you'll get the same care, the same honest advice and a warm welcome every time you visit us in Bridgend.

Questions, Answered

Everything you might want to know before you book.

How do I book an appointment?

The easiest way is by email. Send us a message at samwebbbarber@hotmail.com telling us which service you'd like and when suits you, and we'll get back to you to confirm your slot.

How much do your services cost?

Message for prices — drop us an email at samwebbbarber@hotmail.com with the service you're interested in and we'll happily send you the details. That way you get the right information for exactly what you want done.

Where are you based?

You'll find us in Bridgend. Email us at samwebbbarber@hotmail.com and we'll happily send you directions and help you find the shop.

Do I need to book, or can I just walk in?

Booking by email is the best way to make sure you get a time that suits you and you're not left waiting. Send us a message before you come and we'll do our best to fit you in.

I'm not sure what style would suit me — can you help?

Absolutely — that's part of the job. Come in and talk it through with your barber. We'll look at your hair type, how it grows and how much time you want to spend styling it, then suggest something that genuinely suits you.

Do you offer beard trims and shaves?

Yes — beard trims, shaping and hot towel shaves are a big part of what we do. You can book beard work on its own, or combine it with a haircut as part of The Full Works.
