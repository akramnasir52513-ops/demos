About the salon

Established in 1972 by Paul Reardon, Hairport Hair has spent over 50 years looking after Merthyr Tydfil's hair. We are a family-run, stylish salon where every stylist is trained in-house — and where the personal touch still matters.

We work with L'Oréal, Kérastase and GHD, and we really know hair: from expert cutting and innovative colour to bespoke treatments, every client leaves feeling confident. Colour appointments are arranged by telephone so we can gather every detail and tailor the service precisely to you.
