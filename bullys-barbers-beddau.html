Precision Haircuts

Every cut starts with a proper consultation — your hair type, growth pattern and how you actually style it at home. Clippers and scissors are worked with precision, the edges are cleaned up sharp, and the finish is styled so you can recreate the look yourself the next morning.

  • Consultation before the clippers start
  • Sharp, clean finish around the edges
  • Styled finish & home-care advice

Skin Fades & Taper Fades

From low tapers to high skin fades, the blend is the whole job — no lines, no steps, just a smooth gradient built gradually and checked from every angle. Tell us how tight you like it, or bring a photo and we will match the style to your head shape.

  • Gradual, line-free blending
  • Low, mid, high & taper options
  • Checked from every angle before you leave

Beard Trim & Styling

A beard is shaped, not just shortened. We tidy the length, define the cheek and neck lines, and finish with care so the shape holds as it grows out. Paired with a cut, it is the full refresh — walk out looking deliberate, not accidental.

  • Shape built around your jawline
  • Cheek & neck lines defined crisp
  • Pairs perfectly with any haircut
