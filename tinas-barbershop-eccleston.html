The Tina's standard

Looking after your cut at home

A great cut should still look great on day ten, not just day one. Here's the simple routine we recommend in the chair — no cupboard full of products required.

🚿

Wash less, rinse more

Most hair doesn't need shampooing every day — two or three times a week is plenty for most people, with a plain rinse in between. Over-washing strips the natural oils that keep hair manageable and your scalp comfortable.

✋

Less product, worked in properly

A pea-sized amount is usually enough. Warm it between your palms until it's clear, then work it through from the back of the head forwards — most people load the front and wonder why it looks heavy by lunchtime.

💨

Dry it in the direction it grows

Two minutes with a hairdryer on a medium heat, brushed or finger-combed in the direction your style sits, makes more difference than any product. It sets the shape while the hair is warm, so it holds through the day.

🪒

Tidy the edges between visits

If your neckline or around the ears gets fuzzy before your next cut, a careful once-over with a razor or trimmer keeps things presentable. Not confident? Book a quick tidy-up instead — we'll always give honest advice first.

📅

Book the next one before it nags you

Short styles and fades are easiest to keep sharp on a regular rhythm — most of our regulars rebook every three to five weeks. Message us on WhatsApp when it starts to feel untidy and we'll find you a slot.

🧢

Be kind after a shave

After a hot towel shave, keep things gentle for the rest of the day: lukewarm water, a simple moisturiser, and no strong aftershave on freshly shaved skin. Your face will thank you for it in the morning.
