Aftercare

Keeping your hair looking its best at home

Great hair does not end at the salon door. At every appointment you will leave with simple, honest aftercare advice — and here are the principles behind it.

Protect your colour

Fresh colour is at its most vibrant in the first few weeks. Gentle, colour-safe washing, lukewarm water and leaving a little longer between washes all help it stay rich and glossy for longer. If you are unsure which products suit your colour, just ask — you will get a straight recommendation, not a sales pitch.

Heat, used kindly

Dryers, straighteners and tongs are part of most routines — the key is using them kindly. A heat protectant before styling, moderate temperatures and giving your hair the occasional heat-free day keeps ends stronger and colour brighter between appointments.

Book the tidy-up

Even the best cut needs maintaining. Regular tidy-ups keep the shape sharp, remove tired ends and stop small problems becoming big ones. When you leave, you will be told honestly when your style will next need attention — no pressure, just a plan.

Balayage, the low-maintenance way

Balayage and highlights are designed to grow out softly, but a gloss or toner refresh and a small trim between main appointments keeps the blend seamless. Ask at your visit what interval suits your hair — it is different for everyone.

Condition is a conversation

If your hair starts feeling dry, brittle or over-processed, say so. Sometimes the right answer is a treatment and a pause on lightening rather than more colour. Your stylist would rather stage your goal over two visits than compromise your hair in one.

Questions between visits?

Not sure how to style a new cut, or whether a fringe would suit you next time? Message on WhatsApp between appointments — advice does not stop when you leave the chair, and there is no charge for asking.

Questions, answered

Anything else on your mind? Message on WhatsApp — a real answer, not a call centre.

How do I book an appointment?

The quickest way is WhatsApp — message 07454 003003 with what you would like done. You can also call the same number, and online booking options (Fresha / Microsoft Bookings) are available — just ask and you will be pointed to the current link.

How much do services cost?

Prices depend on your hair length, thickness and the service involved, so they are confirmed individually. Call or message for prices — you will always know the full cost before your appointment is booked, with no surprises on the day.

Do I need a consultation before colour?

For a big change, corrective work or if your hair history is complicated, a consultation is recommended first so the right plan (and the right appointment length) can be set. For straightforward colour, a quick chat by message is usually enough to book you in correctly.

Do I need a patch test?

Colour services may require a patch test in advance, particularly if you are new to colour or have not had colour applied for a while. When you book, you will be told clearly if one is needed and how to arrange it.

I am not sure what style suits me — can you help?

Absolutely — that is what the consultation is for. Bring photos of styles you like (and ones you do not), and you will get honest advice on what will work with your hair type, face shape and how much styling time you realistically have each morning.

How long will my appointment take?

It depends on the service — a cut and finish is far quicker than a full balayage. When you book, you will be given a realistic time estimate so you can plan your day, and appointments are never double-booked or rushed.

Do you cut gents' hair as well?

Yes — gents cuts, crops, fades and restyles are all available. Message or call to book in the same way.

Where is the salon?

The salon is in Neath, Neath Port Talbot, South Wales. Message on WhatsApp or call when you book and you will be given the exact location details and the easiest way to find it.

Can I send a photo of my hair before booking?

Yes — it genuinely helps. A photo of your hair as it is now, plus a photo of the result you are hoping for, lets the salon advise on the right service, the right appointment length and an accurate price before you book.

What should I do before a colour appointment?

Arrive with your hair in its normal state and be ready to talk through your colour history — previous colours, box dyes and treatments all affect what is possible. If a patch test is needed, it must be done in advance, and you will be told exactly how when you book.

What if I need to rearrange?

Life happens — just message on WhatsApp as soon as you can and a new time will be sorted. The earlier you let the salon know, the easier it is to offer your slot to someone else.
