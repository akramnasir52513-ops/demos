Skin fades look sharpest with a tidy-up every 2–3 weeks, while longer and scissor-cut styles can stretch to 4–6 weeks. We'll happily advise based on your style when you're in the chair.

Aftercare

Keep it looking sharp at home

A great cut should keep working for you between visits. A few simple habits make all the difference.

TIP 1

Less product, better placed

A pea-sized amount worked through dry hair beats a handful slapped on top. Start small — you can always add more.

TIP 2

Dry in the direction it sits

Towel-dry or blow-dry your hair the way you want it to lie. Two minutes in the morning keeps the style we cut behaving all day.

TIP 3

Don't wash it daily

Washing every day strips the natural oils that keep hair manageable. Every other day suits most styles — ask us what suits yours.

TIP 4

Book the tidy-up early

Fades grow fast. Message us when you're booking and we'll pencil in your next tidy-up, so you never catch the awkward in-between stage.
