From the Salon Chair

Our Simple Hair Care Guide

Salon hair doesn't have to end when you leave. These are the honest basics we share with our clients every day.

🧴

Protect Your Colour

Coloured hair is happiest with gentle, sulphate-free shampoo and lukewarm water. Wash a little less often, and your shade will stay richer for longer between appointments.

🔥

Always Use Heat Protection

Straighteners and tongs are the biggest cause of dry, snapping ends. A heat-protection spray before styling is the single easiest habit for healthier hair.

✂️

Don't Skip the Trim

Regular trims stop split ends travelling up the hair shaft. Even if you're growing your hair, a light tidy keeps it looking thick and healthy instead of straggly.

💧

Condition Like You Mean It

Work conditioner through the mid-lengths and ends — not the roots — and leave it a few minutes before rinsing. Once a week, a deep mask makes a real difference.

🌙

Be Kind Overnight

Never sleep on wet hair if you can help it, and a loose plait or a silk pillowcase reduces friction, frizz and breakage while you sleep.

❓

Not Sure? Ask Us

Every head of hair is different. At your appointment we'll happily build a simple routine around your hair type, your colour and your budget — just ask.

Frequently Asked Questions

Anything else? Just message us on WhatsApp — we are happy to help.

How do I book an appointment?

The quickest way is WhatsApp — tap any WhatsApp button on this page and send us a message with what you'd like and when suits you. You can also book via Fresha or message us on Facebook.

Where is Envy Salon?

We are at 123b High Street, Redcar, North Yorkshire — right in the centre of town. Message us on WhatsApp if you need help finding us.

How much will my appointment cost?

Prices depend on your hair length and thickness and the service you choose, so we quote individually. Message us on WhatsApp describing what you'd like (a photo helps) and we will give you a clear price before you book.

Do I need a patch test for colour?

Patch tests are required for some colour services, especially if you are new to colour or haven't had it done with us before. Message us before booking colour and we will let you know what's needed.

What is balayage, and is it right for me?

Balayage is a hand-painted highlighting technique that creates soft, natural-looking brightness with a gentle grow-out — meaning fewer root appointments. It suits most hair types and lengths; we will happily advise during your consultation.

Can you style hair for weddings and special occasions?

Yes — we offer occasion styling including up-dos and polished waves for weddings, proms and parties. We recommend booking ahead for popular dates, and a trial appointment is a great idea for weddings.

My hair is damaged. Can you help?

Yes. Our conditioning and repair treatments are designed for dry, stressed or colour-treated hair, and we will always give you honest advice about what your hair can handle and how to get it back to its best.

What are your opening hours?

Opening hours can vary, so the best way to check today's availability is to message us on WhatsApp — we will let you know the latest appointment times.

Can I get advice before committing to a big change?

Absolutely. Send us a message (and a photo of your hair and your inspiration, if you like) and we will talk honestly about what's achievable, how many visits it may take and how to maintain it.

How do I look after my colour between visits?

Use gentle, colour-safe products, go easy on heat styling, and book toners or glossing treatments between colour appointments to keep your shade fresh. Ask us in the salon and we'll recommend a simple routine for your hair.

Do you offer gift vouchers?

A salon visit makes a lovely gift. Message us on WhatsApp and we will let you know the best way to treat someone to a cut, colour or styling appointment at Envy.
