The Keratin Journey

Thinking about a keratin blow-dry? Here's how it works, from first chat to glossy finish.

1. Consultation

We look at your hair's type and condition and talk honestly about the result a keratin treatment can give you, and how it will fit your routine.

2. The Treatment

The keratin formula is applied through the hair and sealed in with heat, smoothing the surface of every strand. The appointment is unhurried — good results take the time they take.

3. The Finish

You leave with hair that is smoother, glossier and far easier to manage day to day — plus our honest advice on looking after it so the finish lasts as long as possible.
