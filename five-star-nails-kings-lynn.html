Your Visit, Step by Step

New to the salon? Here's exactly what happens at your appointment.

1. Consultation & Preparation

We start with a quick chat about what you'd like — shape, length, colour and finish. Old polish or product is gently removed, nails are shaped and cuticles tidied, preparing a clean, healthy base for your new set.

2. The Treatment

Your acrylics, gel or treatment are applied with care, layer by layer. For nail art, each design is painted by hand nail by nail — take your time choosing colours; we'd rather get it exactly right than rush it.

3. The Finish

A final check of every nail, cuticle oil, and a hand massage to finish. You'll leave with care advice to keep your nails looking their best for as long as possible — and our WhatsApp if you need anything.
