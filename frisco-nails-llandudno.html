BIAB — grow your own nails, stronger

BIAB (builder in a bottle) is one of the kindest ways to wear long nails. A layer of builder gel is applied over your natural nail, adding strength and a gentle structure that helps your own nails grow underneath without snapping.

  • Great for weak, peeling or bitten nails
  • Looks completely natural — wear it sheer or with colour on top
  • Infill appointments keep the growth journey going
  • Soak-off removal is gentle when done properly — never prised off

Acrylics — length and drama, built to last

If you dream of long nails today — not in three months — acrylic extensions deliver. We sculpt each nail by hand to suit your fingers and lifestyle, then finish with gel colour and any art you fancy.

  • Almond, coffin, square, oval or stiletto shapes
  • Hard-wearing for hands-on jobs and busy lives
  • Repairs and infills available to keep your set fresh
  • Safe removal in the salon protects your natural nails

Aftercare

Keep your new set looking fresh for longer

A few small habits make a big difference to how long gel, BIAB and acrylic nails last. Here is the advice we give every client at the desk:

  • Cuticle oil daily — keeps the nail flexible and the skin around it healthy
  • Gloves for housework — hot water and cleaning products are polish's worst enemy
  • Nails are jewels, not tools — avoid opening cans and picking at labels with them
  • Never peel or pry — if a nail lifts, message us instead of pulling it off
  • Book infills in good time — regular maintenance keeps sets strong and safe

Prices depend on the treatment, length and design you choose, so we do not list fixed prices here. Call or message us for prices — tell us what you have in mind (a photo helps!) and we will give you a clear quote before you book, with no obligation.

What is the difference between gel, BIAB and acrylics?

Gel polish is a long-lasting colour applied to your own nails. BIAB (builder gel) adds a strengthening layer over your natural nails so they can grow. Acrylics are sculpted extensions that add instant length and strength. If you are not sure which suits you, message us a photo of your nails and we will advise honestly.

How long do gel and BIAB nails last?

With normal care, gel polish typically looks fresh for around two to three weeks, and BIAB sets are usually infilled every three to four weeks as your nails grow. Using cuticle oil daily and wearing gloves for housework really helps.

Can you recreate a nail design from a photo?

Usually, yes! Send us the photo on WhatsApp before your appointment so we can confirm the design, allow the right amount of time, and give you an exact price. Hand-painted art, French tips, chrome and ombré are all popular requests.

Do you remove nails done at another salon?

Yes — safe removal is always better than picking or prying at home, which damages the natural nail. Let us know when booking if you need a removal as well, so we can allow enough time. Call or message for prices.

I bite my nails — can you still help?

Absolutely, and you are not alone. BIAB is a brilliant option for nail-biters because the strong layer protects your nails while they grow underneath. We will never judge — we will help you get nails you are proud to show off.

Do you do nails for weddings and special occasions?

Yes — bridal parties, proms, holidays and photoshoots are some of our favourite appointments. Message us with your date and ideas, and we will plan your set (and your party's, if you like) so everything is ready in good time.

Treatment Guide

Gel, BIAB or acrylics — a simple guide

Three brilliant options, three different jobs. Use this quick guide to find your match — or simply message us and we will recommend the right one for your nails and lifestyle.

💅

Choose gel polish if…

Your natural nails are a length you already like, and you mainly want colour that lasts. Gel polish gives a glossy, even finish that stays put for weeks without chipping the way ordinary polish does. It is the quickest appointment of the three and a great first step into salon nails.

🌱

Choose BIAB if…

Your nails are weak, bendy, peeling or bitten, and you want to grow them. The builder-gel layer works like a protective shield while your own nails grow underneath. Many clients start on BIAB and never look back — their natural nails end up longer and stronger than they ever managed alone.

✨

Choose acrylics if…

You want length and shape right now. Acrylic extensions are sculpted to order — long almonds for a glamorous look, neat squares for something classic — and they are the strongest option for anyone whose hands work hard. Perfect for occasions, and for anyone whose natural nails simply will not grow.
