The Craft

Barbering, Done Properly

Anyone can own a pair of clippers. The difference is in the details — and details are the whole job here.

The Consultation Comes First

Every appointment starts in the chair, not with the clippers. We look at how your hair grows, where it sticks up, how it sat last time it grew out — then we agree the cut before a single blade moves.

  • We ask how you style it day to day
  • We flag what will and won't work with your hair type
  • We agree the length and the finish up front — no surprises

The Finish Is the Reputation

A cut isn't done when the bulk is off — it's done when the edges are clean, the neckline is tidy and it looks right from every angle. That's the part people notice a week later, and it's the part we never skip.

  • Final mirror check from all sides before you leave
  • Simple styling advice so it looks right at home too

Clean Tools, Fresh Blades, Every Time

Hygiene isn't a selling point — it's the bare minimum, and we treat it that way. Tools are cleaned and sanitised between clients, blades are fresh, and the shop is kept as sharp as the cuts.

What's the difference between a low, mid and high fade?

It's where the shortest part of the fade sits. A low fade starts just above the ear and keeps more length on the sides; a mid fade sits higher and looks sharper; a high fade goes up towards the crown for the boldest contrast. Not sure? Sit in the chair and we'll recommend the one that suits your head shape and hair type.

What happens in a hot towel shave?

Hot towels soften your beard and open the pores, then a rich lather is worked in before a straight-razor shave — with the grain, then tidied against it where needed. A cold towel at the end closes the pores and finishes the service. It's the closest shave going, and it's as relaxing as it sounds.

Do you cut children's hair?

Yes — kids are welcome aboard. We keep it patient and relaxed, whether it's a first haircut or a back-to-school tidy-up. If your child is a bit nervous, mention it when you book and we'll do our best to give you a calm appointment.

Can you copy a haircut from a photo?

Bring the photo — it's a great starting point. We'll look at your hair type and growth pattern, tell you honestly how close we can get to it, and adjust the style so it works on your head, not just the model's.

How often should I come back to keep a fade sharp?

Most lads find a fade looks its best for the first couple of weeks and stays tidy for a while after that — how fast it grows out depends on your hair. When you book, ask us and we'll give you a realistic return time for your cut, so you can plan ahead.
