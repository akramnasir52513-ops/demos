About the Shop

The Humble Barbershop is a classic, relaxed barbershop on Gower Road in Killay, Swansea. The barber behind it brings 18 years in the hair industry, having lived and worked in Swansea, Cardiff and Carmarthen, and built a full diary with a waiting list along the way.

No rush, no noise for its own sake — just a proper cut, a friendly word if you want one, and silence if you prefer it.
