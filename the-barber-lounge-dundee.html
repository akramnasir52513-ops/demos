About The Barber Lounge

The Barber Lounge in Dundee was created around a feeling: that a trip to the barbers should slow you down in the best possible way. Warm wood, comfortable chairs, hot towels and unhurried conversation — it is grooming as a proper break in your day, not another errand to rush through.

Whether you pop in for a tidy-up between meetings or book The Full Works as a treat, you will get the same warmth and attention. We listen first, cut second, and only let you leave when you are genuinely happy with what you see in the mirror.

Think of your appointment as a small reset in the middle of everything else: a comfortable seat, a warm welcome, a hot towel if you fancy it, and barbers who genuinely enjoy making people feel good about how they look. Plenty of our regulars tell us it is the calmest part of their week — and we take that as the finest compliment of all.

We are based in Dundee. Email us at thebarberlounge166@hotmail.com and we will happily send you directions.

Do I need to book, or can I walk in?

Booking by email is the best way to be sure of a relaxed, unhurried slot at a time that suits you. Send us a message and we will look after the rest.

Absolutely, and we enjoy it. Mention it when you email or when you arrive, and we will talk through options based on your hair type, face shape and daily routine.

The warm towels are actually there to soften and prepare the skin, which most people find very comfortable. Let us know about any sensitivities when you book and we will take extra care.
