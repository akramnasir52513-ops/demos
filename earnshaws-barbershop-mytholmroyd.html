Barber's advice

Keeping it sharp between visits

A few habits that keep a good cut looking good for longer.

Refresh fades little and often

A skin fade looks its best in the first fortnight. Rather than letting it grow out completely, a quick tidy-up visit keeps the blend clean and is quicker in the chair.

Look after your beard daily

Comb it through, keep the neckline where we set it, and do not carve the cheek lines higher at home — small daily care beats a big rescue job every time.

Less product, better placed

Work a small amount of product through dry or towel-dried hair with your fingers, starting at the back. You can always add more — you cannot easily take it out.

Bring a photo if it helps

Not sure how to describe the style you want? A photo on your phone is genuinely useful — it helps us match the cut to your hair type and how it grows.

Book ahead for busy times

Weekends and the run-up to holidays fill up fast in a popular local shop. A WhatsApp message a few days ahead is the surest way to get the slot you want.

First haircut? No stress

For little ones, a calm first visit matters more than a perfect cut. Bring them at a quiet time if you can, and we will take it at their pace.

How much do services cost?
We keep prices fair and straightforward. Because services vary — a quick tidy-up is a different job from the full works — just call or message for prices and we will tell you exactly what your visit will cost before you book.

You will find us at 13 New Road, Mytholmroyd, HX7 5DZ — in the upper Calder Valley, a short distance from Hebden Bridge, and handy for Luddendenfoot, Cragg Vale and Midgley.
What are your opening hours?
Hours can vary, so the surest way to catch us is to message on WhatsApp or call 07999 089961 and we will confirm the best time for your visit.
Do you do skin fades?
Yes — high, mid, low and taper fades are a speciality of the shop. Every fade is blended in stages and finished with razor-sharp edging around the hairline, ears and neck.
What is a hot towel shave like?
It is the traditional cut-throat shave: steamed towels to soften the beard, a brush-worked lather, a close shave in careful passes, then a cold towel and soothing balm to finish. Allow yourself a relaxed half-hour or so.

Of course. Every appointment starts with a quick consultation, and we will be honest about what will suit your hair type, face shape and daily routine. Bringing a photo of a style you like is always helpful.
Do you trim and shape beards?
Yes — beard trims and shaping include evening out the length, sharpening cheek and neck lines, razor edging and hot-towel softening where needed, so your beard is easier to maintain at home.
