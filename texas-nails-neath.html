How much do treatments cost?

Prices depend on the service and the design you'd like, so the simplest thing is to call or message us for prices — tell us what you're after and we'll confirm the cost before you book. No obligation, no pressure.

What's the difference between acrylic, gel and BIAB?

Acrylics add length and strength using extensions — best for a dramatic change. Gel polish gives natural nails a hard-wearing, glossy colour. BIAB (builder gel) adds a protective layer that helps your own nails grow stronger underneath. Still unsure? Message us and we'll recommend the best fit for your nails.

How long will my nails last?

With good aftercare, most sets look their best for two to three weeks before infills are due, depending on the service and how fast your nails grow. Avoid using your nails as tools, wear gloves for cleaning, and a little cuticle oil each day goes a long way.

Can you recreate a design from a photo?

Absolutely — that's one of our favourite things to do. Send us the picture on WhatsApp when you book, and we'll let you know how we'll create your version of the look.

Do you remove old sets and repair broken nails?

Yes — safe removal matters, because picking off old product damages the natural nail. Message us on WhatsApp to arrange a removal or a repair, and we'll look after your natural nails underneath.
