Welcome

A little luxury on Lands Lane

Tucked into Lands Lane in the heart of Leeds city centre, Luxury Nails has earned its place among the city's three best nail salons — rated 4.6 by ThreeBestRated after a 110-point inspection covering reviews, reputation and service quality.

The salon offers everything from standard manicured acrylics to dazzling gel polish designs, using branded products and skilled, friendly technicians who make every visit feel important. Relax in a modern atmosphere, pick your shade, and let the team take care of the rest. Appointments are booked by phone — walk-ins are welcome when chairs are free.
