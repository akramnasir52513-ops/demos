Questions, answered

Do I need to book in advance?

Booking ahead is best, especially on Fridays and Saturdays — call or message and we will find you a slot.

What should I bring to my appointment?

Just yourself. If you have a photo of a design you love, bring it along and we will match it as closely as we can.

How long does a gel manicure last?

With normal care, gel polish usually lasts two to three weeks without chipping.

Do you do nail art?

Yes — from classic French tips to detailed designs. Tell us what you have in mind when you book.
