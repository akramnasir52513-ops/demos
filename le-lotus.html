Welcome

A little luxury on the High Street

Le Lotus Maidstone is a luxury nail salon dedicated to first-class service. Settle into the lounge bar with a drink while our qualified team look after your nails — guests are welcomed with a coffee on arrival.

Every treatment starts with a pre-consultation, so your nails get exactly the care they need, and our technicians are known for their attention to detail — bring in a design and they'll recreate it for you.

Gift vouchers are available too, valid for six months — an easy treat for someone else, or for your next visit.

Good to know

Before you visit

  • Pre-consultation first: a short consultation is part of every booking, so we only ever treat healthy nails.
  • Cash payments: Le Lotus is cash-only at the moment — the card machine is unavailable, so please bring cash.
  • Age guide: gel polish from 14, acrylic & SNS from 16 (from the salon's own booking terms).
  • Booking ahead is recommended at busy times — call 01622 933088 to secure your slot.
