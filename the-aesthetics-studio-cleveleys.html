In detail

A closer look at the treatments

What each treatment involves, who it tends to suit, and what to expect — the honest version, before you ever sit in the chair.

Anti-wrinkle treatments, explained

Expression lines — across the forehead, between the brows, around the eyes — deepen over the years as the skin loses elasticity. Anti-wrinkle treatments work by relaxing the specific muscles that crease the skin, giving the area a smoother, more rested appearance while keeping your natural expressions.

Suitability, areas and amounts are always agreed at consultation first. Nothing is rushed and nothing is done on the same day unless it is genuinely right for you.

  • Consultation and facial assessment first
  • Conservative amounts — build gradually
  • Review and aftercare guidance included in the conversation

Call or message for prices

Dermal fillers, explained

As we age, the face naturally loses volume, which can leave features looking flatter or more tired. Dermal fillers gently restore that volume and support — commonly in areas such as the lips, cheeks and around the mouth — with results that are visible straight away and soften in naturally over time.

The studio's approach is deliberately understated: small amounts, placed with care, reviewed with fresh eyes. The compliment we aim for is "you look well", never "what have you had done?"

  • Focus on balance and proportion
  • Gradual, buildable approach
  • Full aftercare advice at your appointment

Call or message for prices

Facials & skin treatments, explained

Skin is not the same from one week to the next, so facials here are not picked from a fixed script. Your skin is looked at first, then the treatment is built around it — deep cleansing, exfoliation, masks and massage chosen for calming, brightening, clarifying or deep hydration as needed.

For longer-term concerns like dullness, uneven texture or congestion, a short course of treatments spaced over time usually achieves far more than a single session — something we can plan together honestly at consultation.

  • Tailored to your skin on the day
  • Suitable as a one-off treat or a planned course
  • Straightforward home-care advice to keep results going

Lash & brow, explained

Well-shaped brows and defined lashes do more for the face than most makeup. Brow shaping and tinting brings structure and balance, while lash treatments lift and darken the natural lashes so eyes look brighter with far less daily effort.

Patch tests are arranged in advance where a treatment needs one, and we will always check suitability before going ahead — it only takes a moment when you book.

  • Brow shaping and tinting for definition
  • Lash treatments for a brighter, wide-awake look
  • Patch test arranged in advance where needed
