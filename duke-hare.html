What we do

Services

Three things, done properly. No published price list — call the shop and we’ll talk you through it.

Men’s Cuts

Precision scissor and clipper work, finished to suit you — whether it’s a tidy restyle or keeping a trusted cut exactly as you like it.

Beard Grooming

Shaping and grooming finished with oil and wax, so your beard leaves looking deliberate — neat edges, healthy shine, no stray ends.

Luxury Shave

Our unhurried luxury shave — the traditional ritual, taken slowly and done with care. The proper way to reset.

The shop

Led by Sarah, built on trust

Duke Hare has traded on Scotland Road since 2008. Owner Sarah brings more than 12 years’ experience to the chair — and to a shop regulars describe as relaxed, informal and easy to be in.

That’s the point of a good barbershop: you know your barber, the welcome is genuine, and you leave looking sharper than you arrived.
