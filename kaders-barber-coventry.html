About Kader's

Kader's Barber Shop sits on Stoney Stanton Road in Coventry, serving the local community with traditional barbering and modern fade work. Walk in, take a seat, and leave looking sharp — whether it's a fresh skin fade for the weekend, a tidy beard shape-up, or a classic cut that never goes out of style.

Every cut is finished with attention to detail: clean lines, even blends and a hot-lather neckline finish where requested. No rush, no shortcuts — just a proper barber's cut.

Questions, Answered

Do I need to book, or can I walk in?

Both work. Booking ahead guarantees your slot at busy times; walk-ins are welcome whenever a chair is free. Call or message us to check.

What should I ask for if I want a skin fade?

Just say skin fade and how high you want it — low, mid or high — and whether you want length left on top. Bring a photo if you have one; it helps us match the look exactly.

Do you cut kids' hair?

Yes — kids' cuts are a regular part of the day here, from first haircuts to school-smart trims and fades.
