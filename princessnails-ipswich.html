How long do gel nails last?

With normal care, gel polish typically looks its best for around two to three weeks before a refresh or infill is due. Avoid using your nails as tools, wear gloves for cleaning, and apply cuticle oil regularly to help your set last beautifully.

Do I need an appointment, or can I walk in?

We recommend booking by email so your time is reserved just for you and there is no waiting. Email us first and we will confirm availability for the day and time you would like.

Can you remove my old gel or acrylics?

Yes — safe removal is available and is always kinder to your natural nails than peeling or forcing product off. Mention your current set when you email to book, so we can allow the right amount of time.
