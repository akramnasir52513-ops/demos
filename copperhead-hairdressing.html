Welcome

A New Chapter on St John’s Road

Copperhead moved to Salon Studios in February 2026 — same stylist, same care, a fresh new setting.

Copperhead Hairdressing is run by Heather, now based at Salon Studios, 207 St John’s Road, Edinburgh. The salon offers the full range of cutting and colour services, from a wash, cut & blow dry to foilyage, babylights and tint.

Appointments are kept personal and unhurried — Heather will talk through what you want, what your hair needs, and how to look after it between visits.

Bookings are taken on the salon WhatsApp and via GetTimely. Messages are the quickest way to reach the salon.
