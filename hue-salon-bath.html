About us

Hue Salon in Bath (est. 2013) fuses South American and European techniques with inspired creativity. The team specialises in reconstructing damaged hair with natural treatments and essential oils, in a salon known for outstanding colour and styling.

Services

Women's & Men's Styling

Expert cutting and styling for women and men, across Bath, Bristol and North Somerset.

Colour & Balayage

Balayage, colour and creative colour work from an experienced colour team.

Damaged Hair Reconstruction

Specialist reconstruction for damaged hair, using natural treatments and essential oils.

Beauty & Finishing

Finishing, styling and care advice to keep your hair looking its best between visits.
