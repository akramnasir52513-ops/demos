From the chair

Keep the cut looking fresh between visits

A great haircut should work for you for weeks, not days. Here's the straight advice the barbers at Icon Men give every client in the chair — simple habits that keep a fade sharp, a beard tidy and your hair doing what it was cut to do.

1

Book the tidy-up early

Fades grow out fastest around the edges. A quick tidy-up between full cuts — neckline, sideburns and around the ears — keeps the whole style looking intentional instead of overdue. Ask about it when you book your next full cut on WhatsApp.

2

Less product, worked in properly

Most men use too much product, all at once, on wet hair. Try a pea-sized amount on towel-dried hair, warmed between your palms first and worked from back to front. You can always add a little more — you can't take it back out.

3

Beards need oil, not luck

A few drops of beard oil after a shower stops the itch, softens coarse hair and keeps the skin underneath healthy. Comb it through with the grain so the shape you left the shop with is the shape that stays all week.

4

Wash less, rinse smart

Daily shampooing strips the natural oils that make a cut sit properly. Unless your job demands it, washing two or three times a week — with a plain rinse on the other days — keeps most men's hair healthier and easier to style.
