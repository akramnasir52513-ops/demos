From the Barbers

Keeping it sharp between visits

A great cut deserves a little looking after. Our honest, no-nonsense guide to keeping your hair and beard right until your next chair.

📅

Know your cycle

Most cuts hold their shape for 3–4 weeks. Fades grow out fastest at the edges, so if you like it crisp, book your refresh at 2–3 weeks — a quick message on WhatsApp and you're in the diary.

🧴

Less product, better placed

A pea-sized amount worked through dry or towel-dried hair beats a handful slapped on wet. Warm it between your palms first, start at the back, and finish with what's left at the front.

🧔

Beard basics

Comb your beard down daily so it trains into shape, keep the neckline where we set it, and don't chase stray hairs with home clippers — one slip undoes the shape. Leave the lines to us.

💈

The night before a big day

Wedding, interview or photos coming up? Book your cut 2–3 days before, not the morning of — cuts settle in after a wash or two and look their most natural. A hot towel shave the day before is the finishing touch.

🧢

Hat hair & helmet hair

If you wear a hat or helmet daily, tell us — shorter, tighter styles survive them far better. We'll cut with your real routine in mind, not just the mirror in the shop.

✂️

Thinking of a change?

Fancy growing it out or trying a new style? Growing out is a journey with an awkward middle — regular tidy-ups of the sides and neckline keep you looking deliberate while the top catches up.

What to expect in the chair

New to Jennyfield Barbers? Here's how a visit runs, so you know exactly what you're walking into.

1 · A proper chat first

Before any clippers come out, we talk: what you want, what you've got now, how your hair grows and how much time you spend on it in the morning. Photos are welcome.

2 · Careful, unhurried work

The cut itself is done properly — blended, balanced and checked as we go. We'd rather take an extra two minutes than send you home with a line that shouldn't be there.

3 · The mirror check

Before the cape comes off, you get the mirror — front, sides and back. Not happy with a bit? Say so there and then; we'd much rather adjust it in the chair.

4 · Styling & upkeep advice

We finish the style so you can see how it should look, and show you how to recreate it at home in a couple of minutes — plus when to come back to keep it sharp.

Good to Know

Questions we get asked

Anything else on your mind? Message us on WhatsApp — we're happy to help.

How do I book an appointment?

The quickest way is WhatsApp — tap any WhatsApp button on this page or message 07747 879398. You can also call the same number. Tell us what service you'd like and roughly when, and we'll confirm your slot.

Do you take walk-ins?

Yes — if a chair is free, you're welcome to sit straight down. At busy times (weekends especially) booking ahead on WhatsApp is the safer bet so you're not left waiting.

How much does a haircut cost?

We keep our prices fair and local, but they can vary by service — a full works package isn't the same as a quick tidy-up. Call or message us for prices and we'll tell you exactly what your cut will cost before you come in. No surprises.

What's the difference between a low, mid and high fade?

It's where the fade starts on the head. A low fade begins just above the ears for a subtle, classic look; a mid fade is the versatile all-rounder; a high fade starts further up for a bolder, sharper contrast. Not sure? Show us a photo and we'll advise honestly.

Can I show you a photo of the cut I want?

Absolutely — photos help a lot. Bring one on your phone and we'll talk through whether it suits your hair type and how much styling it needs at home, then tailor it to you.

Do you cut children's hair?

Yes, kids are very welcome. We keep it patient and unhurried, whether it's a first-ever haircut or a smart trim before school photos. Message us to book a time when the shop is a bit quieter if your little one is nervous.

What is a hot towel shave like?

It's the traditional barbering ritual: hot towels soften your beard and open the pores, then a rich lather and a careful straight-razor shave, finished with a cool, soothing touch. It gives the closest shave possible and most customers find it properly relaxing.

Can you tidy up my beard without taking the length off?

Of course. A shape and tidy keeps your length but sorts the lines, removes stray bulk and makes the whole beard look deliberate. Just tell us how much — if anything — you want off.

How often should I come back to keep my cut sharp?

For most cuts, every 3–4 weeks keeps things tidy; skin fades look their best with a refresh around every 2–3 weeks. We'll happily advise based on how your hair grows.

We're your local barber shop in Jennyfield, Harrogate, North Yorkshire. Opening times can vary, so the best way to check today's availability is to message us on WhatsApp or call 07747 879398 — we'll let you know straight away.
