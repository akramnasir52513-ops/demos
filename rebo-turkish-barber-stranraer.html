From the Barber's Chair

Style Guide & Grooming Know-How

Not sure what to ask for? Here's a plain-English guide to the terms we use in the shop, so you can walk in knowing exactly what you want — and walk out with a style you can actually maintain.

Understanding Fade Levels

A skin fade blends the hair from bare skin up into the length on top. A low fade keeps the blend close to the neckline and ears — subtle and professional. A mid fade starts around temple height and suits most head shapes. A high fade takes the blend up towards the crown for maximum contrast against the top. If you're unsure, bring a photo — we'll match the level to your hair and how fast it grows.

Taper vs Fade — What's the Difference?

People mix these up all the time. A taper gradually shortens the hair around the neckline and sideburns only, leaving more length everywhere else — a classic, natural finish. A fade blends over a much larger area of the head and usually goes shorter. Tapers grow out softer; fades look sharper for longer at first but need more frequent tidy-ups to stay crisp.

Getting the Most From a Hot Towel Shave

The hot towel isn't just for comfort — the heat and steam soften coarse beard hair and open the pores, which lets the razor glide closer with less irritation. Arriving with a day or two of growth gives the best result. Afterwards we apply a cold towel to close the pores and a moisturiser to calm the skin, so you leave smooth rather than sore.

Beard Care Between Visits

A shaped beard stays sharp for weeks if you look after it: brush it daily in the direction of growth, keep the neckline clean with careful razor work at home, and use a drop of beard oil to stop the skin underneath drying out. When the cheek lines start to soften and the shape loses its edge, that's the sign to book back in — a quick tidy is always easier than a full reshape.

How Often Should You Book In?

As a rough guide: skin fades look their best with a refresh every 2–3 weeks; classic scissor cuts hold their shape for 4–6 weeks; beards benefit from a professional reshape every 3–4 weeks. Your hair's growth speed matters most — if you're not sure, ask in the chair and we'll tell you honestly when to come back, not before.

First Visit? What to Expect

Come as you are — no need to wash your hair specially or bring anything. We'll start with a chat about the look you want, check your hair's growth patterns and cowlicks, and agree the plan before any cutting starts. Throughout the cut we'll show you progress in the mirror, and at the end we'll style it and explain the one or two simple steps to recreate the finish at home.
