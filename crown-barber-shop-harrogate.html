In Detail

Services, Explained Properly

Not sure which service you need? Here is exactly what happens with each of our most popular services, so you know what to expect before you sit down.

The Skin Fade, Decoded

A skin fade starts at, or very close to, the skin at the sides and back, then gradually blends upward into the longer hair on top. The magic is in the blend — there should be no visible lines or steps, just a smooth gradient. A low fade keeps more weight around the edges and suits classic, professional looks. A mid fade is the versatile middle ground most customers choose. A high fade takes the blend up further for a sharper, more modern contrast. When you book, tell us which you prefer — or bring a photo and we will match the fade to your hair growth pattern and head shape, because the right fade on the wrong head shape simply does not work, and we will always tell you honestly.

Fades grow out faster than classic cuts because the gradient is so precise. Most of our customers return every two to three weeks for a tidy-up, and many book their next appointment before they leave the chair so the fade never loses its edge.

The Hot Towel Shave Ritual

There is a reason the hot towel shave has survived every grooming trend of the last hundred years. The heat and moisture from the towels soften the beard hair and open the pores, which means the razor glides closer with less irritation. We then work up a proper lather, shave with the grain and then against it for the closest possible finish, and complete the ritual with a cold towel and soothing products to close the pores and calm the skin. You will leave with skin that feels smoother than any home shave can achieve — and most customers tell us the relaxing ritual itself is half the reason they come.

Beard Shaping, Not Just Trimming

The difference between a beard that looks grown and a beard that looks groomed is shaping. We consider your face shape, your jawline and how your beard actually grows — including the patches and swirls everyone has — then define clean cheek lines, a tidy neckline and balanced length. A shaped beard frames the face, looks intentional from every angle and, done properly, grows out far more neatly between appointments. If you are growing your beard out, regular shaping visits are the secret to getting through the awkward stages without giving up.

Look After It

Crown Grooming Guide

A great cut is half the job — the other half is what you do at home. These are the tips we give customers in the chair.

✂

Keep the Fade Fresh

Sleeping on a fade flattens one side first — a quick rinse and a damp-hand restyle in the morning revives the shape. Avoid heavy, greasy products on a fresh fade; a small amount of matte product keeps the texture without the shine.

🧔

Beard Care at Home

Wash your beard with warm water daily and a proper beard wash a few times a week. Comb it in the direction of growth while damp, and keep the neckline tidy between visits — but leave the cheek lines to us if you are unsure.

💈

Choose a Style That Fits Your Life

Be honest about your morning routine. If you have five minutes, choose a low-maintenance cut that works with your natural growth. If you enjoy styling, we can give you more to work with on top. The best haircut is the one you can actually recreate.

📸

Bring a Photo

A picture removes all guesswork. Send it on WhatsApp when you book, or show us in the shop. We will tell you honestly how the style will translate to your hair type — and adjust it so it genuinely suits you.

🗓

Book Before the Big Day

For weddings, photos and big events, get your cut three to five days beforehand — fresh cuts settle in and look their most natural just after the first wash or two. Message us early for weekend slots.

👦

First Haircuts for Kids

Bring little ones when the shop is calm and they are rested and fed. A first visit can even just be a sit in the chair and a tiny tidy — building the habit early makes every future cut easier.
