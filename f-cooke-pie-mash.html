Eel merchants since 1862

Yell describes Cooke's as a family business that has served its original recipe for over 160 years — the brand was established in 1862, when pie, mash and eels were the fast food of London.

The Bishop's Stortford shop carries that same name and tradition: traditional pie & mash and liquor, stewed eels, and pies to eat in, take away or stock the freezer with.
