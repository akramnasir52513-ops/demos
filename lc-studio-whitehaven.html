Good to know

Getting the most from your treatment

Before you come

Arrive with clean skin where you can, and avoid heavy sun exposure before facials or waxing. For tinting treatments, a patch test is needed in advance — message us and we will arrange it in good time.

After waxing

Keep the area cool, clean and out of strong sun for the first day, and skip hot baths and perfumed products while the skin settles. We will go through everything at your appointment so you know exactly what to expect.

After facials & massage

Drink some water, take the rest of the day gently if you can, and let the products do their work before applying makeup. Your skin may glow a little afterwards — that is the treatment working.

Looking after nails

Use cuticle oil daily, wear gloves for washing up and cleaning, and never use your nails as tools. Book your infill or removal with us rather than picking — your natural nails will thank you.

A closer look at the treatment room

What actually happens during three of our most-loved appointments — so you know exactly what you are booking.

The signature facial, step by step

We begin by looking at your skin properly — in good light, with no makeup — and talking about what you would like to improve. Then comes a thorough double cleanse, a gentle exfoliation matched to your skin type, and a treatment mask chosen on the day. While the mask works, you get a slow massage of the face, neck and shoulders — most clients drift off here. We finish with serums and moisturiser suited to your skin, and a simple home-care suggestion if you would like one.

  • Skin consultation built into every facial
  • Products chosen for your skin on the day, never one-size-fits-all
  • Massage included as standard, not as an add-on

Lash lifts & tints, explained

A lash lift gently curls your natural lashes upwards so your eyes look wider and more awake — no extensions, no mascara needed. Add a tint and the effect is even stronger, particularly if your lashes are fair at the ends. Brows are shaped with care to flatter your face, and tinted to a shade that suits your colouring. The whole thing is comfortable and relaxing, and we will always patch test first where one is needed.

  • Natural lashes only — nothing glued on, nothing to maintain daily
  • Patch testing arranged in advance where required
  • Shades mixed to suit you, from soft and natural to defined

Occasion makeup, without the stress

Tell us about the event, your outfit and the look you have in mind — photographs help, so feel free to send them over WhatsApp before the day. We will build a look that photographs beautifully and still feels like you: skin that looks like skin, eyes that hold up all day, and a finish designed to last from first photo to last dance. Many brides and guests pair makeup with a facial in the week before, plus nails and lashes, for the full works.

  • Looks planned together in advance — no surprises on the day
  • Long-wear products chosen to last the whole event
  • Combine with facials, nails and lashes for a complete package

Frequently asked questions

How do I book an appointment?

The quickest way is WhatsApp — tap any green button on this page or message 07542 741237. You can also call the same number, or book through our Book in Beautiful / Facebook pages. Tell us the treatment you would like and a rough time that suits you.

Where is LC Studio?

You will find us at 49 Haig Ave, Whitehaven, Cumbria. If you are not sure of the way, message us on WhatsApp and we will happily send directions.

How much do treatments cost?

Prices depend on the treatment and the options you choose, so rather than guess we would rather quote you properly: call or message for prices and we will give you an exact figure before you commit to anything.

What are your opening hours?

Appointments are arranged by booking, so hours flex around clients. Message us on WhatsApp with the day and time you have in mind and we will do our best to fit you in.

Do I need a patch test?

For tinting and some lash treatments, yes — a patch test is needed at least 24–48 hours before your first treatment of that kind. It only takes a moment, and we will arrange it when you book.

It is my first facial — what should I expect?

A friendly chat about your skin first, then cleansing, exfoliation, a mask and massage, all explained as we go. There is no discomfort involved — most clients drift off. Your skin may look a little pink straight after, which settles quickly.

Does waxing hurt?

There is a brief sting as each strip is removed, but it fades immediately and most clients are surprised how manageable it is. We work gently and quickly, and the results last far longer than shaving.

Can I book for a wedding or special occasion?

Absolutely — occasion and bridal makeup are available, and many clients pair it with nails, lashes or a facial in the run-up. Message us as early as you can with your date so we can plan your appointments together.

What if I need to move my appointment?

Life happens — just message us on WhatsApp as early as you can and we will re-arrange your appointment. The earlier you let us know, the easier it is to find you another slot.

I am nervous about trying a new studio. Can I just ask questions first?

Of course — that is exactly what WhatsApp is for. Ask anything at all, with no obligation to book. We would rather you feel completely comfortable before you ever walk through the door.
