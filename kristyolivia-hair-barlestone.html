The Colour Journey

New to colour at KKO? Here's exactly how it works, from first chat to final finish.

1. Consultation

We start with a proper chat about the shade you want, the technique that suits your hair, and how much upkeep you're happy with. Your colour is priced at this consultation — so you know exactly where you stand before anything begins.

2. Skin Test

At least 48 hours before your colour appointment, you'll pop in for a quick skin test. It only takes a moment, and it's there to keep you safe — no colour appointment goes ahead without one.

3. Your Appointment

On the day, your colour is applied with care, developed, rinsed and finished with a blow-dry — so you walk out seeing the full result, not a half-done version of it.

Afterwards: we'll talk you through looking after your colour at home — how often to book refresh appointments and how to keep the shade looking its best between visits. Just ask at your appointment.
