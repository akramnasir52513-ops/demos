About Wee Rebel

Wee Rebel Barber is Perth's small shop with the big personality. We started with a simple grudge: too many haircuts in this world are boring, rushed and exactly the same as the person in the chair before. So we do the opposite — bold where you want bold, classic where you want classic, and careful everywhere, every time.

Rebel is about attitude, not gimmicks. The welcome is friendly, the banter is free, and the standard in the mirror at the end is deadly serious. First visit or fiftieth, you get the same energy: tell us what you are dreaming of, and watch us take it seriously.

And yes — the tartan runs through more than the decor. There is a proud Scottish streak in everything here: do it yourself, do it your own way, and do not let anybody tell you your hair has to look like everybody else's. Bring the idea you have been sitting on for months. The chair is ready when you are.
