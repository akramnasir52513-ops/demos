Questions & Answers

Everything you might want to know before you book.

How do I book an appointment?

Email us at fin@finnyfoofars-beautybox.co.uk or call 07542 200784 — tell us which treatment you'd like and when suits you, and we'll confirm your appointment. As a private home studio, all visits are by appointment.

What is BIAB, and is it right for me?

BIAB (Builder In A Bottle) is a strengthening gel applied over your natural nails to add structure and protection while they grow. It's our specialism — with over ten years of experience — and it's a great choice if your nails are weak, break easily, or you're growing them. Message us and we'll happily advise.

How much do treatments cost?

Message for prices — email fin@finnyfoofars-beautybox.co.uk or call 07542 200784 and we'll confirm the price for the treatment you'd like.

Where is the studio?

We're a private home studio at 111 Kings Avenue, Loughborough LE11 5HX. Because it's a home studio, please book in advance rather than just popping in.

Are you qualified?

Yes — City & Guilds qualified, with over ten years of experience in nails and beauty, specialising in BIAB.

Can I book nails and brows together?

Of course — many clients combine treatments in one visit. Just mention everything you'd like when you message, and we'll arrange an appointment that fits it all in.
