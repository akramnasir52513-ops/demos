About the salon

A salon Salisbury comes back to

Right in the heart of Salisbury on Milford Street, Salisbury Nail Salon has built its name the honest way — one set of nails at a time. With a 4.6-star rating from 266 Google reviews, it is one of the city's most reviewed nail salons, and regulars keep returning for the same reasons: careful work, long-lasting finishes and a friendly, welcoming chair.

Whether you want a classic manicure, a full set of acrylics, dipping powder that holds for weeks, or an ombré set with a little extra flair, the team takes the time to get the shape, length and colour exactly right.
