Aftercare & Occasions

Make your set last — and make it an occasion

A beautiful set deserves a little looking after. Follow these simple tips between appointments and your gel, acrylics or BIAB will stay glossy and strong for longer.

✦

Use your nails as jewels, not tools

Opening cans, peeling labels and prising lids are the fastest way to chip or lift a set. Reach for a tool instead — your nails will thank you.

✧

Cuticle oil, every day

A drop of cuticle oil morning and night keeps the skin around your nails supple and helps your set stay flexible rather than brittle.

♥

Gloves for chores

Hot water, cleaning products and gardening are hard on polish. Pop gloves on for washing up and housework to protect the shine.

★

Book infills in good time

As your nails grow, the balance of an extension shifts. Booking infills every 2–3 weeks keeps sets safe, neat and looking freshly done.

♛

Never peel or pick

Peeling off gel or acrylic takes layers of your natural nail with it. If a nail lifts, message us — a proper soak-off removal is quick and gentle.

☏

Not sure? Just ask

Anything look or feel different between appointments? Send us a photo on WhatsApp and we'll advise honestly — that's what we're here for.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I book an appointment?

The quickest way is WhatsApp — tap any WhatsApp button on this page or message +44 7883 838686. You can also book via Fresha or message us on Facebook. Tell us the service you'd like and your preferred times, and we'll confirm your slot.

How much do treatments cost?

Prices depend on the service, length and design, so we don't publish a fixed list here — call or message for prices and we'll give you an exact quote before you book, with no obligation.

What's the difference between gel, acrylics and BIAB?

Gel polish is a long-lasting colour cured under a lamp, worn on natural nails or extensions. Acrylics are sculpted extensions that add length and strength. BIAB (builder gel) is a strengthening layer over your natural nails that helps them grow. Message us and we'll recommend the best fit for your nails and lifestyle.

How long will my nails last?

It varies with your nails and how you use your hands, but most clients book infills or a fresh set every 2–3 weeks to keep everything looking perfect. We'll advise on aftercare at your appointment to help your set last as long as possible.

Can you do nail art from a photo I bring?

Very often, yes! Send us your inspiration photo on WhatsApp before your appointment so we can plan the design and book the right amount of time for it.

Do you remove gel and acrylics safely?

Yes — we offer gentle soak-off removal that protects your natural nails. Please don't peel or force off your set at home, as that damages the nail underneath; book a removal with us instead.

What's included in a luxury pedicure?

Our luxury pedicure treatment extends the classic pedicure with a longer soak, exfoliating scrub, a nourishing mask, hot towels and an extended massage — finished with the polish of your choice.

Do you do bridal or group bookings?

We love occasion nails — weddings, proms, hen parties and holidays. Message us with your date and how many sets you're dreaming of, and we'll plan it with you.

Where are you located?

We're in Scarborough, North Yorkshire. Message us on WhatsApp and we'll happily point you in the right direction and help with any questions before your visit.

I've never had my nails done — where do I start?

Welcome! A classic manicure or a natural BIAB set is a lovely first step. Message us, tell us you're new, and we'll look after the rest — no question is too small.

Can I match my fingers and toes?

Absolutely — matching (or deliberately clashing!) fingers and toes is one of our favourite requests. Book a manicure or gel set together with a pedicure and we'll coordinate the colour, finish and art across both so everything works as one look.

What should I do before my appointment?

Just arrive with your inspiration photos if you have them, and let us know in advance if you'll need an old set removed — that way we can book enough time. If you're running late or need to rearrange, a quick WhatsApp message is all it takes.
