Healing & aftercare

Looking after your new look

Great results are a team effort — beautiful work in the clinic, and a little care from you at home. You'll leave every appointment with clear aftercare guidance, but here's the shape of it.

The First Few Days

Right after treatment, colour looks stronger and bolder than the healed result — that's completely normal. There may be a little tenderness or tightness.

  • Keep the area clean and dry
  • Don't panic about the darker colour
  • Sleep on a clean pillowcase

The Healing Week

As skin heals, light flaking is common and colour softens considerably. This is where patience matters most — let everything settle naturally.

  • Never pick or scratch flakes
  • Skip makeup on the area for now
  • Avoid saunas, swimming & heavy workouts

Simple Aftercare Rules

You'll be given the exact aftercare for your treatment before you leave. The golden rules are always the same:

  • Follow your aftercare sheet to the letter
  • Use only the products recommended
  • Protect healed PMU with SPF in the sun

The Perfecting Session

Once fully healed, a follow-up session fine-tunes shape and colour and fills any little areas that faded unevenly — the finishing touch that makes the result.

  • Booked once healing is complete
  • Colour and shape refined together
  • Questions answered in person

Good questions

Before you book

The things clients ask most. Anything else — just message and ask.

Does permanent makeup hurt?

Most clients are pleasantly surprised. Numbing products are used before and during the treatment to keep you comfortable, and any sensation is usually described as a light scratching or tingling. Appointments are unhurried, with breaks whenever you need one.

How long does permanent makeup last?

PMU is a long-lasting treatment, but it is designed to fade gradually and gracefully over time rather than stay harsh forever. How long it lasts depends on your skin type, lifestyle and sun exposure — many clients enjoy their results for a good long while before choosing a colour refresh. Your consultation will cover what to expect for your skin.

Will it look natural?

Yes — that's the whole point here. Every design is mapped to your bone structure and agreed with you in the mirror before we start, using colours matched to your skin tone and hair. The aim is "you, but more polished", never a stamped-on look.

What happens at a consultation?

We'll talk about the look you want, look at styles and shapes together, and check that the treatment is suitable for you. You'll get honest advice, a clear idea of the process and healing, and a price before you decide anything. There's no obligation to book.

How much do treatments cost?

Prices depend on the treatment and what's right for you, so they're confirmed personally rather than guessed at on a website. Call 07753 112346 or message on WhatsApp for prices — you'll get a straight answer and no pressure.

What is a fine line tattoo?

Fine line tattoos are small, delicate designs created with a single needle for thin, elegant lines — things like tiny flowers, words, dates and symbols. They're a lovely choice for a first tattoo or a meaningful little piece. Bring your idea to a consultation and we'll design it together.
