The ritual

The Turkish Experience

New to a Turkish shave? Here's exactly what happens, step by step.

1. The Hot Towels

Steaming towels are wrapped around your face to open the pores and soften the beard. It relaxes the skin and prepares it for the closest possible shave — and for many customers, it's the best part of the week.

2. The Razor

Rich lather is worked in with a brush, lifting every hair. Then the straight razor does its work — slow, precise strokes for a finish no electric shaver can match.

3. The Finish

A cold towel closes the pores, followed by soothing balm. No burn, no tightness — just smooth, fresh skin and that unmistakable just-shaved feeling.
