About us

A relaxing nail bar on Queen Street

Cici Nail Bar offers customers excellent, high-quality nail services in a relaxing spa setting, right in Wrexham town centre at 21 Queen Street.

The salon provides an extensive selection of manicure and pedicure treatments, and its certified nail technicians guarantee a consistent, high-quality experience for every customer.

Cleanliness is a point of pride: the salon sets new standards by using newly sanitised tools for each client, and the friendly, well-trained team aims to deliver the same quality on every visit.
