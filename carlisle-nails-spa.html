As general guidance, gel polish commonly lasts around two to three weeks depending on your nails and how you use your hands. We will share aftercare tips at your appointment to help your set last as long as possible.

Absolutely — photos are a great starting point. Bring or email your inspiration and we will talk through how it can be adapted to your nail shape and length.
