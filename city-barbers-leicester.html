About City Barbers

On Rutland Street in Leicester city centre, trusted by generations of customers.

City Barbers has been part of Leicester life since 1987. That is decades of sharp cuts, honest advice and friendly faces — the kind of shop where regulars have been coming for years and new customers are welcomed like old friends.

From classic short back and sides to modern fades and careful beard work, every cut is done with time and pride. Walk in, take a seat, and leave looking your best.
