Welcome to Cameo

Hair & Tanning, Done Properly

A friendly, professional salon where you are never rushed — and always listened to.

Cameo Hair & Tanning is known locally for its excellent service and friendly team. The salon specialises in haircuts and tanning, with a professional yet welcoming atmosphere that helps every client relax from the moment they walk in.

Stylists including Chris, Georgie and Bee are praised again and again in reviews for listening carefully and taking real care and attention — whether it is a complete restyle or your regular cut and finish. Under the same roof you will also find our treatments studio for gel nails, waxing, brows and lashes, and our state-of-the-art lie-down sunbed.

You will find us at Unit 2, Beacon Retail Park — easy to reach, with free parking, and dogs are welcome too.
