Welcome to 182 South

Where Giffnock comes to feel amazing

From bold colours and fresh cuts with Schwarzkopf and Color Wow, to restorative treatments with K18 and Keratin Revolution — your hair goals are covered.

Thinning hair? Book a consultation with our in-house trichologist. We also offer hair extensions for all hair types.

For beauty, there are custom skin treatments powered by Dermalogica, flawless nails with Nail Order, full body waxing, spa body treatments and holistic therapies such as Reiki.

Transformation or well-earned self-care — our talented team is here to make you feel amazing.
