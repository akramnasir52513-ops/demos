About Greenleaf

Situated in Great Barr, Birmingham, Greenleaf Treatments offers state-of-the-art treatment rooms and a beauty section designed specifically to meet your needs. Our talented team of beauty therapists provides professional hair care, male grooming for him and beauty therapy for her — with an extensive product range so every treatment can be tailored to you.

Why clients love us

  • Therapists trained to a very high standard, with regular external training
  • A free consultation before every treatment, so it's tailored to your needs
  • Swedish, aromatherapy and deep tissue massage, plus group bookings for weddings and hen & stag parties
  • Low prices and free parking, just off the Aldridge Road
