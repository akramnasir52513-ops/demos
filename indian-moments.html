Menu Highlights

The dishes our guests mention most in their reviews. This is a taste of the menu — call us for today's full menu.

Starters & From the Grill

Guest favourite

King Prawn Puri

King prawns in a flavoursome spiced sauce, served on a soft fried puri bread — the starter reviewers keep naming.

Onion Bhaji

Crisp, golden onion fritters — described in reviews as "huge" and "fantastic".

Tandoori Mixed Grill

A sizzling assortment straight from the clay oven — tandoori chicken, tikka and kebabs to share.

Poppadoms & Chutney

The classic way to start — crisp poppadoms with the chutney tray while you choose.

Mains

Guest favourite

Lamb Dansak

Tender lamb cooked with lentils in a sweet, sour and fairly hot sauce — a house regular's pick.

Chicken Dansak

The same lentil-rich dansak sauce with chicken — warming heat, rounded with a touch of sweetness.

Lamb Rogan Josh

Made with fresh tomatoes, the lamb tender and full of flavour without being too spicy, as one reviewer put it.

Chicken Tikka Madras

For heat lovers — tikka pieces in a fiery madras sauce. Reviews confirm: it brings the heat.

Rice & Breads

Pilau Rice

Fragrant spiced rice — the essential partner to any curry.

Mushroom Rice

Rice stir-cooked with mushrooms — ordered alongside the dansak in one of our favourite reviews.

Cheese Naan

Pillow-soft tandoor naan with a melted cheese filling — made for scooping up every last bit of sauce.
