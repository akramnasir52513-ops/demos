Our Services

Time-honoured barbering with a modern eye — every appointment begins with a consultation, as it should.

Haircuts

A proper haircut, the traditional way: a conversation first, then careful scissor and clipper work shaped to your head and hair. Whether you favour a timeless classic or something more current, the finish is checked from every angle and styled so you can manage it easily at home.

  • Consultation before every cut
  • Classic and contemporary styles
  • Styled finish, checked all round

Skin Fades

The modern favourite, barbered with traditional patience. Your fade — low, mid or high — is blended step by step for a smooth, even gradient that melts into the skin, with tidy edges to complete it. No rushing, no shortcuts: just a clean fade that holds its shape.

  • Low, mid and high fades
  • Gradual, even blending throughout
  • Tidy edges and neckline finish

Beard Trim & Hot Towel Shave

The hot towel shave is barbering’s oldest ritual, and we still do it properly: hot towels to soften the beard and open the pores, a close cut-throat shave, and a soothing finish. Beard trims shape and tidy your beard to suit your face, with clean lines throughout.

  • Traditional hot towel preparation
  • Close, comfortable cut-throat shave
  • Beard shaped with clean, tidy lines

Questions & Answers

All you need to know before your visit.

How do I book an appointment?

Booking is by email — simply write to andysbarbershop@outlook.com with the service you would like and a time that suits you, and we shall confirm your appointment.

What are your prices?

Please message for prices when you email — we will confirm the price for your chosen service before your visit, plainly and with no obligation.

Where is the shop?

You will find us in Luton. Email us at andysbarbershop@outlook.com for directions and we shall be glad to help you find your way.

Must I book, or may I walk in?

Walk-ins are very welcome whenever a chair is free. Even so, emailing ahead is the surest way to secure your preferred time.

I am unsure what style would suit me — can you advise?

Certainly. Bring a photograph if you wish, or simply describe what you have in mind, and your barber will recommend a style suited to your hair and face at your consultation.

Do you offer beard trims and hot towel shaves?

Yes indeed — beard trims and traditional hot towel shaves are a speciality of the house, available alone or with a cut in The Full Works.
