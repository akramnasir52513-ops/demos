Welcome to Muskaan

Muskaan Nails & Beauty in Wembley has all you need to pamper your nails with a manicure, pedicure or extensions — or treat your skin to a facial or wax. The modern salon has a warm, welcoming atmosphere, and the team of expert beauty therapists and nail technicians consult with you before every treatment, so it is tailored to exactly what you want.

Choose from a comprehensive menu — luxury gel manicures, BIAB builder gel, acrylic and gel extensions, lash tinting, threading or waxing — with leading products from brands such as OPI, CND, Dermalogica and Crystal Clear.
