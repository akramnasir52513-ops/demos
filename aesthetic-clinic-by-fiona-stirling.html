About the clinic

The Aesthetic Clinic by Fiona Sutherland is a multi award-winning clinic in Stirling, easily reached from Glasgow, Edinburgh, Perth, Fife and Dundee. Owner Fiona Sutherland (RGN, NIP) is a Facial Aesthetics Specialist and Nurse Independent Prescriber, with a large emphasis on safety and natural-looking results.

Every treatment starts with an in-depth consultation — Fiona tailors each plan to you, never rushes a decision, and only uses the highest-quality products, including Botox®, Juvederm, Voluma, Volbella, Volift, Restylane and Teosyal.
