About the salon

One stop for hands, lashes and beauty

On Old Christchurch Road, a short walk from Bournemouth town centre, Lily's Nail Bar has built a loyal following — 4.4 stars from 211 Google reviews and counting. Customers come for the nails and stay for everything else: lashes, waxing, tanning and massage, all under one roof.
