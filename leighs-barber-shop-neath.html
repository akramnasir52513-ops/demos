Looking after your cut between visits

A good haircut should keep working for you long after you leave the chair. A few simple habits make the difference between a cut that lasts a fortnight and one that still looks sharp a month later.

First, less is more with product: a pea-sized amount worked through dry or slightly damp hair beats a handful slapped on wet. Second, dry your hair in the direction you want it to sit — two minutes with a hairdryer does more than any gel. Third, keep the edges tidy: if the neckline and around the ears start to look fuzzy before the rest needs cutting, that is your sign to book the next appointment.

For beards, a brush through each morning and keeping the cheek and neck lines where we shaped them will hold the shape between trims. And if you are ever unsure which product suits your hair, just ask at your next visit — honest advice is part of the service.

  • Use a small amount of product, worked through properly
  • Dry hair in the direction you want it to fall
  • Book again when the edges fuzz — not when it is overgrown
  • Brush beards daily to hold the shape we cut
