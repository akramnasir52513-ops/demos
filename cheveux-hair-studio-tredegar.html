In detail

A closer look at what we do

Choosing a service is easier when you know what actually happens in the chair. Here's the honest detail behind our most-booked appointments.

Colour, done properly

Great colour is a process, not a product. It starts with looking at your hair's history — previous colour, heat habits, condition — then choosing a shade that works with your skin tone rather than against it. Roots-only regrowth appointments keep all-over colour looking fresh between full services, and toners refresh faded lengths and ends so the whole head reads as one beautiful, even colour. If your hair has been through a lot, we may suggest a staged plan: sometimes the most glamorous route is the patient one, and we'd rather your hair stayed strong while it gets lighter.

The perfect cut is a conversation

A haircut should solve problems, not create them. Before scissors come out, we talk about how your hair falls naturally, where it misbehaves, how you dry it, and how long you actually spend styling (be honest — we design for real life). Fringes, layers, texture, length — every element is chosen to grow out gracefully, so your hair still behaves weeks later, not just on day one. And before you leave, we style it the way you'll do it at home, talking you through each step as we go.

Styling for the days that matter

Some days just ask more of your hair: weddings, christenings, proms, interviews, photographs you'll keep forever. Occasion styling at Cheveux is planned, not improvised — bring your outfit photos and inspiration pictures, and we'll build a look that suits the day, the dress and you. From soft romantic curls to sleek polished finishes and classic up-dos, the aim is always the same: hair that looks wonderful at 9am and is still holding beautifully at midnight.

The Cheveux hair guide

Three things your hair wishes you knew

1. Your hair has a history. Every colour, every heat tool, every summer of sunshine is still in the lengths of your hair. That's why honest consultations matter — the plan has to respect what's already happened to your hair, not just what you'd like next.

2. Condition is the real luxury. Shiny, swishy hair isn't about the most expensive service — it's about healthy hair, well cut and properly cared for. Sometimes the glossiest result comes from a great cut and a conditioning routine, not another chemical process.

3. The best style is the one you'll actually do. A look that needs forty minutes and three tools will last exactly one day in most lives. Tell us your real routine and we'll design for it — beautiful hair should fit your morning, not fight it.

Aftercare

Keep the salon feeling at home

Protect Your Colour

Use colour-safe shampoo, wash in lukewarm (not hot) water, and leave a day or two between washes where you can. Dry shampoo is your friend on day two.

Heat, Gently

Always use a heat protectant before straighteners or curling tools, and keep the temperature as low as does the job. Your ends will thank you.

Little & Often Trims

Regular small trims keep ends healthy and shapes sharp, so your style never reaches the "I can't stand it" stage. Ask at your visit when to rebook.

Questions

Before you book

How do I book an appointment?
The quickest way is WhatsApp — message 07507 185893. You can also message the studio on Facebook or book through Fresha. Tell us what you'd like done and a rough time that suits you.
How much will my appointment cost?
Prices depend on your hair's length and thickness and exactly what's involved — a full colour on long hair is simply more work than a fringe trim. Call or message for prices and we'll give you a clear quote before you commit to anything.
Where is the studio?
We're at 83 Ashvale, Tredegar, in Blaenau Gwent, South Wales — easy to reach from across Tredegar and the surrounding valleys.
I'm not sure what I want — can I just get advice?
Absolutely, and honestly that's the best way to start. Message us a photo of your hair as it is now and anything you've seen that you like, and we'll talk through what's achievable and what it would involve.
What's the difference between balayage and highlights?
Highlights are foiled sections that lighten hair from root to tip for an all-over brighter effect. Balayage is painted on freehand, concentrated through the mid-lengths and ends, so it grows out softly with no obvious regrowth line — lower maintenance, very natural.
Will colour damage my hair?
Any chemical service asks something of your hair, which is why the consultation matters. We'll assess your hair's condition first, recommend the gentlest route to your goal, and sometimes suggest building up lightness over more than one visit.
Do you do wedding and occasion hair?
Yes — occasion styling is one of our favourite things. For weddings, message as early as you can so we can plan trials and timings for the morning itself.
How often should I get my hair cut?
It depends on your style — short, sharp cuts hold their shape for a shorter time, while longer styles can go longer between visits. We'll give you an honest recommendation for your specific cut at your appointment.
What if I need to rearrange?
Life happens. Just message on WhatsApp as soon as you know, and we'll do our best to find you another slot that works.
Why don't you have a price list on this page?
Because hair isn't one-size-fits-all, and a guessed price helps nobody. Message us what you're after and we'll quote you properly — it only takes a minute.
I'm growing my hair — should I still come in?
Yes, and it's one of the smartest things you can do. Growing hair still needs its ends tidied and its shape maintained, otherwise it just gets longer and shaggier rather than longer and lovelier. We'll trim minimally and keep the shape working at every length along the way.
My hair is fine and flat — can a cut actually help?
Very often, yes. The right shape removes weight from the wrong places, adds movement, and makes fine hair look noticeably fuller. Pair it with the right blow-dry technique — which we'll show you in the chair — and the difference can be dramatic.
Can I bring my child for a haircut?
Message us and ask — we're a small, friendly studio and happy to talk through what you need. Tell us your child's age and what you're hoping for, and we'll let you know the best way to book it.
What should I do before a colour appointment?
Arrive with your hair as it normally is — no need for a special wash the night before unless we ask. If you've used home colour recently, tell us honestly when you book; it genuinely changes the plan, and knowing upfront protects your hair and your result.
