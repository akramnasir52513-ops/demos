How long will my nails last?

Gel typically lasts 2–3 weeks with care. Extensions usually need infills after 2–3 weeks of growth. Moisturising your cuticles and avoiding using nails as tools will help any set last longer.

Do I need an appointment?

We recommend booking by email so we can reserve a calm, unhurried slot just for you. Message us to see this week's availability.

Can you remove my old gel or acrylics?

Yes — we offer safe removal services that protect your natural nails. Please never peel or bite product off; book a removal instead and we will do it gently.
