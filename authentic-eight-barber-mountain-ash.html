Look after it

Keep the cut looking fresh

Making a fade last

A fade looks its sharpest in the first week or two, then softens as it grows. A few habits stretch the fresh look further:

  • Brush or comb the top into shape while it's slightly damp
  • A pea-sized amount of product is plenty — more just weighs it down
  • Book your tidy-up before it starts to feel overgrown, not after

Beard care basics

Between studio visits, a little upkeep keeps the shape we cut in:

  • Comb it through daily to train the growth direction
  • Tidy stray hairs only — leave the lines to us at your next trim
  • Hot water opens it up; a cooler rinse keeps it comfortable

Not sure what to ask for?

Bring a photo. Screenshots of styles you like are the fastest way to get exactly the cut you're picturing — and if it won't suit your hair type, we'll say so honestly and find the version that will.

Big occasion coming up?

Weddings, interviews, holidays and nights out all deserve the Full Works. Book a few days ahead of the event itself so the cut has settled in and looks completely natural in the photos.
