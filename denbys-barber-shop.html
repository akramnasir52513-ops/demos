Our Heritage

Est. 1997

Denby’s Barber Shop has been trading on Cleveland Road, High Barnes, since 1997 — serving the people of Sunderland for well over two decades.

That kind of staying power only comes from doing the job properly: good cuts, fair dealing, and a shop where regulars and first-timers alike are made welcome.

The shop is rated 5.0 on ThreeBestRated, which lists Denby’s among the best barbers in Sunderland.
