About the salon

Paul Allan Hair & Beauty has been established for over 25 years in Glasgow’s West End. Paul and his team are L’Oréal trained, and Paul himself is a certified balayage master who is also graduating as a L’Oréal colour specialist.

Colour correction is the salon’s main service — the team are highly skilled at fixing unwanted results, including box dyes. They also specialise in balayage techniques such as airtouch, teasy lights and French balayage, in perms, and in hair extensions including the mesh integration system.

There is a beauty & treatment room at the same address, and every visit starts with a warm welcome and your choice of hot tea or coffee.
