How long do gel nails last?

With normal care, gel polish typically looks its best for around two to three weeks before a refresh or infill is due. Use cuticle oil daily, wear gloves for housework, and avoid using nails as tools to keep them looking glamorous for longer.

Do I need an appointment, or can I walk in?
