Our Services

Whatever your hair needs, we have got you covered — and every service starts with a friendly chat about what you would like.

Haircuts

Your everyday cut, done with real care. We start by asking how you like your hair and how you style it day to day, then cut it to suit your hair type and face shape. Every angle is checked before the finish, and we will show you how to style it at home so it keeps looking good between visits.

  • Friendly consultation at every visit
  • Cuts for classic and modern styles
  • Simple styling tips to take home
Email for prices — just ask when you book

Skin Fades

Clean, smooth fades are one of our most popular asks. Choose low, mid or high and we will blend it gradually into your chosen length on top, keeping the gradient even and the edges neat. The result is a fresh, tidy look that is easy to keep smart as it grows out.

  • Low, mid and high fades
  • Even, gradual blending
  • Neat edges and a tidy neckline
Email for prices — just ask when you book

Beard Trim & Hot Towel Shave

Let us take care of your beard properly. We trim it to a shape that suits you, tidy up the lines, and finish with products that keep the hair soft and the skin comfortable. Our hot towel shave softens everything first for a close, smooth and relaxing finish.

  • Beard shaped and tidied
  • Hot towels for comfort and closeness
  • Soothing, conditioning finish
Email for prices — just ask when you book

The Full Works

The complete tidy-up in a single visit: your haircut or fade, plus a beard trim or hot towel shave, all finished and styled. It is perfect before a special occasion — or whenever you fancy walking out feeling completely fresh. One appointment and you are done.

  • Your choice of haircut or fade
  • Beard trim or hot towel shave
  • Fully styled finish included

Questions & Answers

A few things people often ask before visiting.

How do I book an appointment?

Just email us at s4j4r@hotmail.com — tell us the service you would like and when suits you, and we will get you booked in and confirmed.

How much will my visit cost?

Please message for prices when you email — we will confirm the price for the service you choose, so you know exactly where you stand.

Where can I find you?

We are in Luton. Email us at s4j4r@hotmail.com for directions and we will happily guide you to the shop.

Do I need to book in advance?

Walk-ins are welcome when chairs are free, but emailing ahead is the safest way to secure the time you want, especially at busy periods.

I am not sure what to ask for — can you advise?

Of course — that is what the consultation is for. Tell us what you like, show a photo if you have one, and your barber will suggest a style that suits your hair and is easy to look after.

Do you offer beard services?

Yes — beard trims and hot towel shaves are available, on their own or together with a haircut as part of The Full Works.
