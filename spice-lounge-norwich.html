Welcome to Spice Lounge

Serving Norwich since 2006 from our home on Wensum Street, a short stroll from Tombland and the Cathedral. Warm, welcoming service and a kitchen built around the classics — tandoori specialities, chef's specials, balti and korahi dishes, traditional Sylheti recipes and biriani, with vegetable sides and tandoori breads baked to order.
