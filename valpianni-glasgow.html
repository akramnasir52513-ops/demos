Cuts & Styling

Whether it is a tidy trim or a complete restyle, your cut starts with a consultation about your hair type, face shape and daily routine. Your hair is washed and conditioned, cut with precision, then blow-dried and finished — so you leave knowing exactly how it will look day to day.

  • Consultation & hair assessment first
  • Wash, cut & blow-dry finish included
  • Styling advice to recreate the look at home

Colour

From natural-looking coverage to a whole new shade, colour is never rushed. Every colour service begins with a consultation so the shade, technique and upkeep are planned around you — and a skin test is carried out at least 48 hours beforehand, so your appointment is as safe as it is beautiful.

  • Personal colour consultation before you commit
  • Skin test at least 48 hours before (required)
  • Shade planned around your skin tone & lifestyle

Blow-Dry & Finishing

A professional blow-dry gives you that smooth, bouncy, just-left-the-salon finish that lasts. Perfect before a special occasion or a big week — or simply for the days you want to feel your very best, washed, blow-dried and styled with care.
