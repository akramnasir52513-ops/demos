Whether you like a clean natural look or a colour that pops against a seaside sky, you will leave with hands that feel soft, neat and ready for anything.

Where are you located?

You will find us in Weston-super-Mare. Email us at alivia_nails@outlook.com for directions and we will help you find us easily.

Booking by email is recommended so your time is reserved and you are not kept waiting. Send us a message first and we will find a slot that suits you.

As general guidance, gel polish typically lasts around two to three weeks, depending on your nails and daily activities. We will give you simple aftercare tips at your appointment to help it last.

Can I book nails and beauty together?

Yes — nails and beauty are under one roof here, so just mention everything you would like in your email and we will arrange your visit together where availability allows.
