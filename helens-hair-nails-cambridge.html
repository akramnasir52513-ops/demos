About the salon

Nails and hair, under one roof in Cambridge

Helen's Hair & Nails is a professional and affordable salon at 25 Burleigh Street, Cambridge CB1 1DG — open every day, so whenever you want gorgeous, fresh nails and hair, the team is here to help.

Choose from manicures, pedicures, full set acrylics, gel powder and BIAB gel, plus nail art in classic shades, shellac, powder and ombre. With a 4.3-star rating from 281 Google reviews, it is one of Cambridge's most-reviewed local salons.

  • Acrylic, gel and BIAB nails with nail art
  • Manicures and pedicures
  • French manicures and chrome finishes
  • Hair services from the same friendly team
  • Open every day — walk-ins and bookings welcome
