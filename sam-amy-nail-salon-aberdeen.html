Where are you located?

You will find us in Aberdeen. Email us at sam_amy_nail_salon@hotmail.com for directions and we will make sure you find us with ease.

Do I need to book in advance?

We strongly recommend booking by email, especially at busy times — with so many returning clients, popular slots fill quickly. Email ahead to secure your preferred time.

How long does gel polish usually last?

As general guidance, gel polish commonly lasts around two to three weeks with normal wear. Good aftercare makes a real difference, and we will talk you through it at your appointment.

Can you recreate a design from a photo?

Very often, yes. Email or bring your inspiration photo and we will discuss how the design can be adapted beautifully to your nail shape and length.
