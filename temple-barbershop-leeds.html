About Temple Barbershop

Temple Barbershop is a barbershop in Leeds built around one simple idea: a good cut, done properly, by barbers who take their time. No rushing, no conveyor belt — a proper consultation, a clean finish, and a shop where regulars are known by name.

From sharp fades to classic cuts, beard work to the full hot towel shave, everything on the services list below is done in-house, in the chair, by people who do this all day, every day.

Inside the shop

Questions, answered

Do I need to book, or can I just walk in?

Walk-ins are welcome whenever a chair is free, but booking by email is the safest way to get the time you want, especially on Fridays and Saturdays.

How much does a cut cost?

Call for prices — prices depend on the service and the barber, and we will confirm before we start.

Do you do skin fades and beard work?

Yes — fades, beard shaping and hot towel shaves are all part of the daily work here.

What should I bring or tell my barber?

Just bring a photo of the style you like if you have one, and tell your barber how you normally style your hair. A short chat first avoids surprises.

Can I book for a group or a special occasion?

Yes — email us with the date and how many people, and we will do our best to fit you in.
