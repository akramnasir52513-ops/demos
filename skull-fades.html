More than a barbershop

Skull Fades is home to the Skullfades Foundation. Owner Ged King — Manchester's "barber to the homeless" — and the team give free haircuts to people living on the streets, run outreach trips, and train disadvantaged men in barbering at the shop.

Every paid cut in the chair helps keep that work going.
