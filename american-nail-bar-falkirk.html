Questions & Answers

Good questions, straight answers.

How do I book an appointment?

By email — send a message to americannailbar12@gmail.com with the service you would like and when suits you, and we will reply to confirm.

How much do treatments cost?

Please message for prices, as it depends on the service and design you pick. Email us your idea and we will confirm the price up front.

Where are you located?

We are in Falkirk. For directions, email us at americannailbar12@gmail.com and we will happily point you our way.

How long will my nails last?

Gel typically lasts 2–3 weeks with care. Extensions grow out naturally and usually need infills every 2–3 weeks to stay looking fresh and feeling strong.

Do I need an appointment?

Booking by email is recommended so your seat at the bar is saved for you. Drop us a line to check this week's availability.

Do you do removals?

Yes — we offer safe gel and acrylic removal that looks after your natural nails. Never peel product off at home; book a removal with us instead.
