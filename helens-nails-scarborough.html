Safe removal, healthy nails

When it's time for a change, removal matters as much as application. Gel and acrylics should always be soaked and eased off — never peeled, pried or bitten away, which strips layers from your natural nail.

Good to know

Getting the most from your nails

Use cuticle oil daily

A drop of cuticle oil each day keeps your natural nails flexible and your gel or BIAB lasting longer.

Nails aren't tools

Opening cans and picking at labels is the fastest way to chip or lift a set — gloves for cleaning help too.

Book infills in time

Most sets need maintenance every 2–3 weeks as your nails grow. Ask when you book.

Never peel them off

Peeling removes layers of your natural nail. Book a proper soak-off removal instead.

A quick guide to choosing your perfect set

New to nail enhancements, or thinking of switching systems? Here's how local clients in Scarborough tend to choose — and remember, you can always ask on WhatsApp for personal advice.

If your nails are short and you want length now

Acrylic extensions are the fastest route to the length and shape you want. They're sculpted or tipped to your chosen shape — almond for elegance, square for a classic look, coffin for drama — then finished with gel colour or art. As your natural nails grow underneath, regular infills keep the set looking fresh, and many clients later switch to BIAB once their own nails have length.

Ask about acrylics

If you want your own nails — just stronger

BIAB is made for you. The builder gel sits on your natural nail like a supportive shield, so the nail underneath can grow without the usual snapping and peeling. The look is clean and natural, the upkeep is simple infills every few weeks, and over a few appointments most clients are amazed at the length they've grown themselves.

What's the difference between gel, BIAB and acrylics?

Gel polish is colour and shine on your natural nails. BIAB (builder gel) adds strength over your natural nails to help them grow. Acrylics create extensions for instant length and the strongest finish. If you're unsure, message with a photo of your nails and we'll advise honestly.

How long will my nails last?

With proper prep and aftercare, gel and BIAB typically look great for 2–3 weeks, and acrylics similar with infills as they grow. Using cuticle oil daily and treating your nails gently (they're not tools!) makes a real difference.

Can you do nail art?

Yes — from French tips, ombré and chrome to hand-painted designs. Art takes extra time, so mention it when you book and share a photo of the design you love so enough time is set aside.

I have weak or bitten nails. Can you still help?

Usually, yes. BIAB is a popular choice for weak nails because it adds a protective, strengthening layer while your natural nails grow underneath. Message the salon about your nails and you'll get honest advice on the best starting point.

Do you remove old gel or acrylics?

Yes, safe soak-off removal is available. Please never peel or pry off old sets yourself — it strips layers from your natural nail. Mention removal when you book so time is allowed for it.
