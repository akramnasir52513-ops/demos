Not Sure What To Book?

Gel, BIAB or acrylic — a quick guide

01

Choose gel polish if…

Your natural nails are already a length you like and you want colour with serious shine. Gel polish is thinner and more natural-feeling, and it’s the quickest appointment of the three — great for busy weeks and holidays.

02

Choose BIAB if…

You’re growing your nails or they break easily. The builder layer adds strength while keeping a natural look, and because it’s soaked off and reapplied (or infilled), your natural nail keeps growing underneath, protected.

03

Choose acrylic if…

You want instant length or a dramatic shape. Acrylics are the hardest-wearing option and the best canvas for long designs. They need infills every few weeks to keep the balance and shape looking right.

Still deciding? Send us a photo of your nails on WhatsApp and tell us the look you’re after — we’ll recommend the best option honestly, even if it’s the simpler one.

Aftercare

Keep your new nails looking their best

A few simple habits make a big difference to how long your set lasts:

  • Apply cuticle oil daily — it keeps nails flexible and the skin healthy
  • Wear gloves for washing up and cleaning — water and chemicals lift product
  • Never use your nails as tools to open tins or pick at labels
  • Book infills every 2–3 weeks for acrylics and BIAB as they grow out
  • If a nail lifts or breaks, message us — don’t glue or peel it yourself
  • Always book a proper soak-off removal instead of pulling product off

Nail Know-How

Words you might hear in the salon

Infill

As your nails grow, a gap appears near the cuticle. An infill appointment refreshes the product at the base, rebalances the nail and keeps acrylics and BIAB strong — usually every 2–3 weeks.

Soak-off

The safe way to remove gel, BIAB or acrylic: product is gently softened and lifted away without forcing, protecting the natural nail underneath. Far kinder than peeling or prying.

French & ombré

French tips pair a natural pink base with crisp white (or coloured) tips. Ombré blends two shades into each other for a soft gradient — both are timeless, and both look beautiful over gel or BIAB.

What’s the difference between gel, BIAB and acrylic?
Gel polish is a long-wear colour on your natural nails. BIAB (builder in a bottle) adds a strengthening layer so your own nails can grow. Acrylics are sculpted extensions for instant length and shape. If you’re unsure, send us a photo of your nails on WhatsApp and we’ll advise.
How long will my nails last?
With normal wear and good aftercare, gel polish typically looks great for 2–3 weeks, while BIAB and acrylics are usually infilled every 2–3 weeks as your nails grow. Cuticle oil and gloves for housework really do help them last longer.
Can you recreate a design from a photo?
Very often, yes. Send us the photo on WhatsApp before your appointment so we can check the colours and techniques needed, quote for the design, and book enough time to do it justice.
Do you remove gel or acrylics applied elsewhere?
Yes — book a soak-off removal. We remove product gently and finish with shaping and conditioning so your natural nails are left in the best possible condition. Please never peel product off, as it takes layers of your natural nail with it.
