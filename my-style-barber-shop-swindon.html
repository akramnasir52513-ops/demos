About us

My Style Barber Shop is a traditional gentleman's barbershop with a modern twist in the heart of Swindon town centre, just a short stroll from the train and bus station. Great haircuts for men and boys of all ages, plus professional hot-towel shaves in a chilled environment with old-school service.

Questions, answered

Do I need to book, or can I walk in?

Booking ahead is best — email us with your preferred day and time and we will confirm your slot.

How do I book?

Email us at the address on this page with the service you want. We reply to confirm.

Do you cut children's hair?

Yes — family appointments are welcome. Tell us the age when you book so we allow the right time.

What should I do if I need to cancel?

Email us as early as you can so we can offer your slot to someone else.
