Questions & Answers

Everything you might want to know before you book.

How do I book an appointment?

Email us at ellasnailhouse@gmail.com — tell us what you would like and when suits you, and we will confirm your appointment. You can also find us on Facebook.

How much will my nails cost?

Prices depend on the service and design you choose. Message for prices — email us with the look you have in mind and we will happily confirm before you book.

I am not sure which service I need — can I ask?

Of course. Email us a photo of your nails or of a design you love, tell us what you are hoping for, and we will advise honestly — no obligation to book.

Do you do nail art?

Yes — from classic French tips to seasonal designs. Bring inspiration photos if you have them, and we will talk through what will work best on your nails.

Where are you based?

We are in Colchester. Email us for directions and to check the latest availability before your visit.

How do I look after my nails afterwards?

We will talk you through aftercare at your appointment — including how to keep your nails looking their best and when to book your next visit.
