Our Services

Proper barbering, done with care. Every service starts with a quick consultation — message us for prices and to book.

Precision Haircut

Every cut starts with a consultation about the style you want, your hair type and how it grows — so the finish suits you, not just the photo. Your barber works with clippers and scissors for a clean, sharp result, checks every angle in the mirror with you, and finishes with styling product and simple advice for keeping the shape at home.

  • Consultation before the clippers come out
  • Scissor & clipper work tailored to your hair
  • Styled finish & at-home care advice

Skin Fade & Taper

Fades are our craft. Whether you want a low, mid or high skin fade, a taper, or a classic short back and sides, the blend is built gradually and checked from every angle — no harsh lines, no rushed edges. Tell us the look you're after, or bring a photo, and we'll match it to your head shape and hairline.

  • Low, mid & high fades plus classic tapers
  • Gradual blend — checked from every angle
  • Photo references welcome

Hot Towel Shave

The traditional way to shave. Your skin is prepared with hot towels to soften the beard, a rich lather is applied, and the shave is done with care in stages — followed by a cold towel and soothing balm to close the pores and calm the skin. You leave smooth, refreshed and properly looked after.

  • Hot towel preparation & rich lather
  • Careful, staged traditional shave
  • Cold towel & soothing balm finish

Beard Trim & Styling

A great beard needs looking after. We shape and tidy your beard to suit your face, sharpen the edges and neckline, and finish with beard oil or balm so it looks neat and feels soft. Whether you keep it short and sharp or long and full, we'll keep it in top condition — and show you how to maintain it between visits.

  • Shape & tidy suited to your face
  • Sharp edges & clean neckline
  • Beard oil / balm finish included

Questions & Answers

Everything you might want to know before you book.

How do I book an appointment?

Just send us an email at gcimageandgrooming@outlook.com — tell us which service you'd like and when suits you, and we'll confirm your appointment. Use the Message Us button anywhere on this page and your email app will open ready to go.

How much does a haircut cost?

Prices vary by service, so please message us for prices — send a quick email and we'll let you know the cost of exactly what you're after, with no obligation to book.

Can I just walk in?

Walk-ins depend on how busy the chairs are on the day. To be sure of your slot — especially at weekends — we recommend messaging ahead by email to book.

What should I ask for if I'm not sure of the style name?

No problem at all. Bring a photo of the look you like, or simply describe it, and your barber at Gentlemens Choice will talk you through what will work best for your hair type and face shape before anything starts.

Do you do skin fades and beard trims?

Yes — skin fades, tapers, traditional hot towel shaves and beard trims and styling are all core services here. See the services section above for full details of each one.

Where are you based?

Gentlemens Choice is based in Swansea. Send us an email at gcimageandgrooming@outlook.com and we'll confirm the best way to find us and book you in.
