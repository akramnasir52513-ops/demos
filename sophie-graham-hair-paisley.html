About the salon

Welcome to your happy place. At Sophie Graham Hair, ‘hairstylist’ means so much more — your new best friend, therapist, your stylist. The salon is an official K18 salon, uses the cruelty-free, PETA-approved Evo colour range (ammonia-free and PPD-free), and offers Cezanne Keratin smoothing — a formaldehyde-free way to smooth it all out.

The team — Sophie, Elle, Hannah, Megan and Beau — are praised by clients for listening properly, nailing cut and colour, and making every visit feel relaxed and welcoming.
