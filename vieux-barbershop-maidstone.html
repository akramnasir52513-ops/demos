About Vieux Barbershop

Vieux Barbershop is a Maidstone barbers built around a simple idea: barbering done the old way still works best. Careful consultation, sharp tools, warm towels and no rushing — the small details that turn an ordinary haircut into an appointment you actually enjoy.

Whether you are a regular who knows exactly what they want or someone trying a new style for the first time, you will get the same patience and honest guidance. Tell us what you are after, and we will make sure you leave happy with what you see in the mirror.

There is a particular pleasure in sitting down in a proper barber's chair: the warm towel, the quiet concentration, the feeling of time slowing down for half an hour while someone skilled simply looks after you. That is the experience Vieux keeps alive in Maidstone — traditional in the best sense, and personal to every single customer who sits down with us.

Everything you need to know before your visit.

How do I book an appointment?

Simply email us at ryan_1212@hotmail.co.uk with the service you would like, and we will confirm your appointment and answer any questions.

How much does it cost?

Message for prices — email us with the service you have in mind and we will happily confirm the price before you book, with no obligation.

Where are you based?

We are based in Maidstone. Email us at ryan_1212@hotmail.co.uk and we will happily send you directions.

Do I need to book, or can I just walk in?

Booking by email is the best way to guarantee your slot and avoid a wait, especially at busier times. Drop us a message and we will find a time that suits you.

I am not sure which style would suit me — can you help?

Of course. Tell us when you email, or simply ask in the chair — we will talk through your hair type, face shape and how much styling time you have, and recommend a style that works for you.

What should I do before a hot towel shave?

Just turn up as you are. The warm towels do the preparation work for you — all you need to do is sit back and enjoy it.
