About Jenna

A luxury bridal hair and makeup artist with more than 20 years of industry experience, working across South Wales, Mallorca and destination weddings worldwide.

Jenna holds the Guinness World Record title for the fastest hair stylist and has won multiple industry awards for business, hair, makeup, weddings and entrepreneurship. Her work spans events, TV, film and advertising, and her celebrity transformations have attracted attention online.

At the Newport salon, a carefully selected collective of independent beauty professionals each specialise in their own area — from hair cuts and colours to nails, brows and beauty treatments — so every client gets a personal, specialised experience. Jenna's theatrical makeup is created entirely by hand, with no AI, digital manipulation or editing used on the facial makeup itself.
