A salon that listens first

Blues HQ is a popular hair salon on Rawson Square in Bradford city centre, built on a simple idea: consult properly, then cut, colour and finish to a high standard — whether it's a major transformation or a routine tidy-up.

The team is regularly trained and updated on the latest trends, and only professional products are used, so you leave with hair that looks great and a care routine that keeps it that way.

Blues HQ also has a separate, private room for ladies who wear the hijab, khimar or niqab, where hair and treatments are done in privacy, with female staff only.
