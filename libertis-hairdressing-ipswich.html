Questions, Answered

Everything you might like to know before your visit.

How do I book an appointment?

Simply email us at libertipauline@gmail.com with the service you would like, and we will confirm your appointment and help with anything else at all.

How much does it cost?

Email for prices — tell us the service you have in mind and we will happily confirm the price before you book, with no obligation whatsoever.

Where are you based?

We are based in Ipswich. Email us at libertipauline@gmail.com and we will happily send you directions.

Do I need a skin test before colour?

Yes — a skin test is required 48 hours before any colour appointment. It only takes a moment, it keeps you safe, and we will arrange it with you when you book your colour by email.

Do I need to book, or can I just pop in?

Booking by email is the best way to secure an unhurried appointment at a time that suits you, especially for colour and occasion hair, which need a little planning. Do get in touch — we would love to hear from you.

I am not sure what style or colour would suit me — can you help?

With pleasure — it is one of our favourite parts of the job. Mention it in your email, and we will talk through your hair, your lifestyle and what you love, then recommend something truly flattering.
