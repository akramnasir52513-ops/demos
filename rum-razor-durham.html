About The Shop

Barbering is a huge part of our life — every haircut is an opportunity to turn a bad day around or boost someone's confidence. We aren't saving lives, but we believe we can support the male society on another level. Everyone is welcome here. Ego's are left at the door — just relax in the shop, the perfect hideaway.

Meet Paul and Tom — barbers who take their time, listen to what you want, and never rush a five-minute short back and sides. An excellent, unrushed haircut, every time.
