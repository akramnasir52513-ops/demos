The Studio

Just You, Your Hair, Your Stylist

Erika Hairstylist is a one-on-one hair studio at 126 Talbot Road, in Colville Square, Notting Hill — a short walk from Westbourne Park, Notting Hill Gate and Ladbroke Grove.

With no conveyor belt of clients, every appointment is unhurried and personal: precision cutting and bespoke styling, tailored to your hair and your routine. The studio is rated 5.0 from 18 reviews on Treatwell.

The best way to get in touch is a text or WhatsApp message to 07472 879459.

Services

Cut · Colour · Finish
