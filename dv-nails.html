About the studio

DV Nails, Church Street

DV Nails is a nail salon in Peterborough city centre, specialising in acrylic and gel nail extensions, manicures and pedicures. The salon takes bookings by phone and shares its latest sets on Facebook.

✓Extensions specialists — acrylic and gel full sets, listed on Fresha for central Peterborough.
