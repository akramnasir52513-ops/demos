About the salon

Capelli brings a luxury, high-end salon experience to the heart of Maesteg town. From a lived-in blonde to a colour correction or a rich, glossy brunette, the team tailors every service to you and communicates with you at all times.

Expect a complimentary drink, a one-to-one consultation with an experienced stylist or colourist, and a personal service from start to finish.
