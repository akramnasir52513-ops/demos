About Lincoln Barber Shop

Lincoln Barber Shop is a local barber shop in the heart of Lincoln, built on a simple idea: a haircut should never feel rushed. When you sit in one of our chairs, you get a barber who listens first — to what you want, what your hair actually does, and what will work for your day-to-day life — and only then picks up the clippers.

Our barbers take real pride in the details: clean blends, tidy edges and a finish you can keep looking good at home without a shelf full of products. Whether you're in for a regular tidy-up, a complete restyle or the full works with beard and hot towel, you'll get the same care, the same honest advice and a warm welcome every time you visit us in Lincoln.

Where are you based?

You'll find us in Lincoln. Email us at mardan1@hotmail.co.uk and we'll happily send you directions and help you find the shop.

Do I need to book, or can I just walk in?

Booking by email is the best way to make sure you get a time that suits you and you're not left waiting. Send us a message before you come and we'll do our best to fit you in.

Absolutely — that's part of the job. Come in and talk it through with your barber. We'll look at your hair type, how it grows and how much time you want to spend styling it, then suggest something that genuinely suits you.

Yes — beard trims, shaping and hot towel shaves are a big part of what we do. You can book beard work on its own, or combine it with a haircut as part of The Full Works.
