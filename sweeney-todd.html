Our heritage

On South Street Since 1887

Sweeney Todd has been trading since 1887 — a heritage barber shop in the heart of Perth, at 85 South Street. Generations of customers have sat in our chairs for the same thing: a proper cut, done by people who take pride in the craft.

From classic cuts to modern skin fades, and from pompadours finished with an open razor to simple beard trims, the approach has never changed — honest barbering, traditional skill, and a warm welcome on South Street.
