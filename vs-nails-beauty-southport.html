Gel Nails

Gel polish is applied over your natural nail or extensions and cured for an instant-dry, mirror-shine result — no waiting around and no smudging your nails on the way out. Choose from French tips, soft nudes, seasonal shades or full-on colour. Gel is a brilliant choice if you want low-maintenance beauty that fits around work, family and everything in between.

BIAB (Builder in a Bottle)

The go-to treatment for growing your own nails stronger and longer. BIAB adds a protective builder layer over your natural nail, helping to prevent breakages and peeling while you grow. It is ideal if your nails are weak, if you are transitioning away from extensions, or if you love that clean, natural look with real durability underneath. What is the difference between acrylic, gel and BIAB?

Acrylic adds length and strength with extensions built by hand. Gel polish is a long-lasting, glossy colour applied over your natural nail or extensions. BIAB (Builder in a Bottle) is a strengthening overlay on your natural nails that helps them grow while protecting them from breakage. If you are not sure, send us a photo of your nails on WhatsApp and we will recommend the best fit for you.

How long will my nails last?

With normal care, gel polish typically looks great for around two to three weeks, while acrylic and BIAB sets are usually maintained with infills every two to three weeks as your nails grow. Lifestyle makes a difference — gloves for housework and cuticle oil daily will help any set last longer. We will give you simple aftercare advice at your appointment.

Can you copy a nail design from a photo?

Very often, yes. Send us the photo on WhatsApp when you book so we can allow the right amount of time and confirm we have everything needed for your design. If a design needs adapting for your nail shape or length, we will talk it through with you first.

Do you offer nail repairs and removals?

Yes — we repair individual damaged nails, provide infills for grown-out sets, and remove extensions safely and gently to protect your natural nails. Please never pull or pry extensions off at home; book a removal with us instead and your natural nails will thank you.

Do lash and brow treatments need a patch test?

Some tinting and lash treatments require a patch test at least 24–48 hours before your appointment, especially if it is your first time having that treatment with us. We will let you know when you book and arrange the quick patch test for you.

What if I need to cancel or move my appointment?

Life happens — just message us on WhatsApp or call us as early as you can, and we will do our best to find you a new time. The earlier you let us know, the more likely we can offer your original slot to someone else.
