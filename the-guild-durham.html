About The Guild

The Guild is owned by Dan Smith and Matt Smith and is known across Durham for friendly, professional service. The team — including barber Lewis — covers everything from children's cuts to scissor cuts, shape-ups and beard work.

There's a simple online booking system so you can grab the slot that suits you, free parking for customers, and gift vouchers available in-store.
