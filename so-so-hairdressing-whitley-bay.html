About the salon

A little haven away from the hustle and bustle, right on Park View. So & So opened in October 2001 after owner Tony spotted a gap in Whitley Bay for a salon rich in customer service, with a tailored, personal approach to the latest cutting and colouring techniques.

The salon moved to its current home at 234 Park View at the end of 2016 — a modern, comfortable space with bespoke fixtures mixed with the building's bold, industrial features, designed entirely by Tony.
