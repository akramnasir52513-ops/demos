The Experience

Inside a traditional Turkish shave

Never had a proper Turkish hot towel shave? Here is how the ritual unfolds — it is as much about slowing down as it is about the shave itself.

Steam & Soften

Hot, steamed towels are wrapped around your face to open the pores and soften the beard, while your skin is prepared with pre-shave care.

Lather & Shave

A rich lather is worked in with a traditional brush, then the cut-throat razor passes with the grain, and again across it, for the closest finish possible.

The Details

Edges are cleaned up, stray hairs are dealt with — including traditional ear flaming — and the neckline is razored crisp and clean.

Cool & Cologne

A cold towel closes the pores, soothing balm calms the skin, and a splash of classic lemon cologne sends you out feeling brand new.

What is a Turkish hot towel shave?

It is the traditional wet shave done properly: steaming hot towels to soften the beard, a brush-worked lather, a cut-throat razor shave, then cold towels, balm and cologne. It is closer and kinder to the skin than a standard cartridge shave.

What is ear flaming — does it hurt?

Ear flaming (also called singeing) is a traditional Turkish technique that quickly burns away fine ear hair with a controlled flame. It takes only seconds, is completely painless, and leaves a much cleaner finish than trimming.

Do you do skin fades?

Yes — low, mid and high skin fades are one of our most popular services. Every fade is blended by hand, foil-finished and checked from every angle before you leave.

Do you cut children's hair?

Yes, we welcome kids and young gents. We keep it patient and relaxed, whether it is a first haircut, a school-ready trim or a teenage fade.

Can you sort out my beard shape?

Of course. A beard sculpt balances the length, sharpens the cheek and neck lines with the razor, and finishes with beard oil — shaped to suit your face rather than a one-style-fits-all trim.
