In Detail

What each treatment feels like

Not sure which massage is right for you? Here's a closer look — or simply message Victoria and she'll help you choose.

For aches, knots and desk shoulders: deep tissue

If your back feels like concrete by Friday, deep tissue is the one. Working slowly with forearms, elbows and firm hand pressure, the treatment targets the deeper muscle layers where long-held tension lives. It can feel intense in the moment — in a good way — and most people notice the difference as soon as they stand up.

It's equally popular with people who train, garden, drive for work or spend long hours at a desk. Tell Victoria where it hurts and the session will focus there.

Ask about deep tissue

For pure calm: Swedish, aromatherapy and hot stone

Some appointments aren't about fixing anything — they're about stopping. Swedish massage uses flowing, rhythmic strokes to settle the whole body; aromatherapy adds carefully chosen essential oils to deepen the calm; and hot stone brings gentle, penetrating warmth that helps you let go of tension you didn't even realise you were holding.

These are the treatments to book when life has been loud — before a big event, after a difficult week, or simply because you deserve an hour where nobody needs anything from you.

Your first massage? You're in safe hands

Lots of clients at Victoria Betts Massage Therapy are first-timers. You'll be talked through everything: what to expect, how much clothing is involved (only the area being worked on is ever uncovered), and how to say if the pressure needs to change. There are no silly questions.

Indian head massage is a gentle place to start — you're clothed or lightly draped, seated or on the couch, and it focuses on the scalp, neck and shoulders where so much everyday tension gathers.

Good To Know

Before & after your treatment

A few simple things that help you get the very most from your massage.

🕐 Arrive a few minutes early

Give yourself a moment to arrive and settle before your treatment starts. A short consultation at the beginning makes sure the session is right for you.

💧 Drink water afterwards

Massage encourages circulation and releases tension from the muscles. Drinking water after your treatment helps you feel the full benefit.

🗣️ Speak up — always

Too firm? Too gentle? Too warm? Say so at any point. The treatment is yours, and small adjustments make a big difference.

🌙 Take it easy after

If you can, keep the rest of your day gentle after a treatment. Many people feel deeply relaxed — that's the point, so enjoy it.

Treatment Guide

Which treatment is right for you today?

Match how you're feeling to the treatment that will help most. And if you're torn between two — ask, and we'll blend elements of both.

😮‍💨 "I'm stressed and can't switch off"

Choose Swedish or aromatherapy massage. Flowing strokes and calming essential oils are designed to quiet a racing mind and reset your whole system. Many clients say this is the best sleep they've had in weeks.

💻 "My back and shoulders are killing me"

Choose deep tissue massage. Firm, focused work on the back, neck and shoulders targets the knots that build up from desks, driving, lifting and training. Add an Indian head massage focus if the tension reaches your scalp and jaw.

🥶 "I feel stiff, cold and tense all over"

Choose hot stone massage. The warmth of the stones encourages muscles to soften and release far more quickly than hands alone, and the heat itself is deeply comforting on a Northumberland day.

🎁 "I want to treat someone else"

Any treatment makes a thoughtful gift — relaxation for a new mum, deep tissue for someone sporty, or pure indulgence for someone who never stops. Message on WhatsApp to arrange it.

🤕 "I get tension headaches"

Choose Indian head massage. Working the scalp, face, neck and shoulders, it targets exactly where tension headaches gather, and leaves your head feeling clear and light.

🌿 "I just need an hour that's mine"

Choose a relaxation treatment. No decisions, no agenda — Victoria will blend techniques into a flowing session shaped around you on the day, and the world can wait outside the door.

How much does a treatment cost?
Prices depend on the treatment and session length you choose. Call or message on WhatsApp for current prices — you'll always know the full cost before you book, with no obligation.
Which massage should I choose?
For general relaxation, choose Swedish or a relaxation treatment. For stubborn knots and aches, deep tissue. For warmth and deep calm, hot stone. For stress held in the head, neck and shoulders, Indian head massage. Still unsure? Message Victoria and she'll recommend one based on how you're feeling.
I've never had a massage before. Is that okay?
Absolutely — many clients are first-timers. Everything will be explained before your treatment, only the area being worked on is ever uncovered, and you can ask for the pressure to change at any time.
What should I wear?
Wear whatever is comfortable. For couch treatments you'll undress to your comfort level for the area being treated and be covered with towels throughout. Indian head massage can be done fully clothed or lightly draped.
Can massage help my bad back or sore shoulders?
Massage is widely used to ease muscular tension, stiffness and aches in the back, neck and shoulders, and many clients book regularly for exactly that. Massage isn't a substitute for medical care, so if you have an injury or a health condition, mention it when you book and Victoria will advise whether treatment is suitable.
Do you offer gift vouchers?
A massage treatment makes a lovely gift. Message on WhatsApp to ask about gift options for birthdays, thank-yous or a treat for someone who needs to unwind.
What if I need to rearrange?
Life happens — just send a WhatsApp message as early as you can and Victoria will do her best to find you another time.
How often should I have a massage?
It depends on your body and your goals. Some people come monthly to keep tension at bay, others book whenever aches build up or they need to switch off. Victoria can suggest a rhythm after your first treatment.
Is there parking nearby?
House of Hair is on Tweed Street in the centre of Berwick-upon-Tweed, close to the town's public car parks and on-street parking. If you're coming by train, the treatment room is a short walk from Berwick-upon-Tweed station. Message on WhatsApp if you'd like directions before your visit.
I'm pregnant — can I have a massage?
Some treatments can be adapted, but suitability during pregnancy depends on your stage and circumstances. Mention it when you message to book and Victoria will advise honestly on what's appropriate for you.
What if I'm running late?
Send a quick WhatsApp message if you're delayed. Victoria will always do her best, though if the day is fully booked your session may need to be a little shorter so the next client isn't kept waiting.
