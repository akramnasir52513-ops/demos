Welcome

A little luxury on Duke Street

Grand Lux Nail Spa is a luxurious salon in the heart of Reading, offering premium nail and spa services in a stylish and relaxing environment. Our experienced technicians specialise in acrylic extensions, BIAB, gel polish, spa manicures, pedicures, and bespoke nail art tailored to your personal style.

We use high-quality products and maintain excellent hygiene standards to ensure every visit is comfortable and enjoyable. Whether you're preparing for a special occasion or simply treating yourself, our friendly team is here to help.
