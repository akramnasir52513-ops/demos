Welcome to the Emporium

A Cardiff institution since 1992

Established in the capital in 1992, Nailcraft quickly became known as the place for outstanding luxury nail treatments and customer service. Today the Emporium's highly qualified nail artists and beauty therapists welcome loyal regulars and new clients alike to our salon inside the Wyndham Arcade — built in 1837, in the heart of the city.
