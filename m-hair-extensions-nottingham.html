About M Hair Extensions

Craft, care and over three decades of extension expertise.

M Hair Extensions sits on George Street in the heart of Nottingham. For over 30 years, the salon has specialised in one thing done properly: beautiful, natural-looking hair extensions, fitted and blended by people who truly know hair.

The salon is the only Great Lengths Supreme Status Salon in the country, and co-founder James Henderson is a Global Ambassador for Great Lengths. M Hair Extensions also works with the Little Princess Trust, donating extensions made into wigs for children.
