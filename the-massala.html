Our Story — Since 1984

The Massala has been serving Borough Road since 1984. The restaurant was founded by Mr Shamal Biswas, and today it is run by Mohammed Ripon, with manager Sajid Hussain — “Saj” — looking after the front of house.

Everything centres on the clay oven and the dish the house is known for: our signature Chicken Tikka Massala. Add tandoori grills, biryanis cooked dum-style and a menu of around 300 dishes, and there is something for every table.

“Quality food, customer satisfaction and value for money is our only aspiration.”

Dine in, collect, or have it delivered — we are open every day from 5pm to 11pm.
