In detail

What happens during your appointment

Never been to Sharon's Barber Shop before? Here is what a typical visit looks like, from the moment you walk through the door on North Street.

The consultation comes first

Every appointment starts with a proper conversation. We will ask what you have in mind, how you usually wear your hair, and how much time you spend on it in the morning. If you have a photo of a style you like, show us — it helps us understand exactly the length, shape and finish you are picturing.

We will also be honest with you. Some styles need a certain hair type or a bit of growing first, and we would rather tell you that upfront and agree a plan than send you home with something that will not sit right by the weekend.

Careful cutting, checked twice

During the cut we work methodically — building the shape gradually rather than taking too much off at once. Fades are blended in stages and checked from every angle, edges are cleaned up carefully around the ears and neckline, and the finish is styled so you can see exactly how it will look day to day.

Before you leave the chair, we hand you the mirror and check the back together. If anything needs adjusting — a little more off the fringe, a tighter blend — that is the moment, and we are always happy to do it.

Beards, shaves and the finishing touches

Beard appointments follow the same unhurried approach: we agree the shape first, take the length down evenly, sharpen the lines around the cheeks and neck, and finish so the beard sits neatly against your face shape. A hot towel shave is the full traditional experience — towels, lather, a close razor finish and a calm, comfortable end to the treatment.

For younger customers, we keep things relaxed and pressure-free. Parents are welcome to stay close by, and we work at whatever pace keeps the child comfortable — a positive first experience at the barber matters far more to us than speed.

Look after your cut

Simple grooming tips from your Ripon barber

A great haircut should keep looking good long after you leave North Street. Here is the straightforward advice we give customers in the chair.

Book before you grow out

Most cuts look their best for the first few weeks. If you like your hair sharp, message us on WhatsApp to book your next visit before the edges start to soften — short back and sides styles especially benefit from regular tidy-ups rather than waiting until everything has grown out.

Less product, better placed

A pea-sized amount of styling product, warmed between your palms and worked through dry or nearly-dry hair, beats a big handful every time. Start at the back and sides, then finish the top — that way the front never ends up overloaded or greasy looking.

Tell us what did not work

If part of your last cut grew out awkwardly — a stubborn crown, a fringe that would not sit — tell us at your next visit. Honest feedback helps us adjust the cut so it behaves better with your hair's natural growth pattern.

Beard care between visits

Comb your beard downwards daily to train the hairs, keep the neckline tidy with careful small trims, and wash it with warm water rather than very hot, which dries the skin underneath. Leave the reshaping to us at your next appointment.

Bring a photo, honestly

Photos help a lot — of a style you like, or even of your own hair on a good day. We will always be straight with you about whether a style will work with your hair type, thickness and face shape before a single cut is made.

First haircuts, gently done

For little ones, a short, calm visit works best. Bring them at a quiet time of day if you can, and do not worry if the first trip is just about getting comfortable in the chair — a good experience matters more than a perfect finish.
