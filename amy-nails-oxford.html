Manicure

It is the treatment that keeps your hands looking well-kept every day — understated, elegant and always appropriate.

We recommend booking by email so that your time is reserved and your appointment is unhurried. Message us first and we will find a suitable time.

As general guidance, gel polish typically lasts around two to three weeks, depending on your nails and lifestyle. We will share aftercare advice at your appointment to help your finish last.

Yes — bring or email a photograph of the design and we will talk through how it can be adapted to your nail shape and length.
