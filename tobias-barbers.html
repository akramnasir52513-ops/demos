Since 1990

A Walsall Institution

Tobias Gentleman's Barbershop has been cutting hair on Station Street since 1990 — long enough that customers who first came as boys now bring their own sons.

The shop specialises in traditional methods with contemporary styling: the barbers believe every haircut reflects the man wearing it, so each cut starts with listening. No rush, no fuss — a warm welcome and a great haircut, every time.

Most of our customers have been with us for years. With dependable service at honest prices, it's easy to see why.

— The Tobias team, 36 Station Street

What We Do

Services

Traditional skills, modern styles — for gentlemen of all ages. Call 01922 474 454 for today's prices and availability.

Gentlemen's Haircut

Scissor and clipper cuts finished with care — classic short back and sides or a modern style, cut to suit you.

Skin Fades

Sharp, clean fades blended to the skin — high, mid or low, finished with precise detailing.

Traditional Wet Shave

The full old-school ritual: hot towels, rich lather and a steady hand, finished with aftershave.

Beard Trim & Shape

Beards trimmed, shaped and edged with the razor for clean lines and a tidy finish.

Clipper & Buzz Cuts

Quick, tidy all-over clipper cuts — sharp, simple and easy to keep.

Children's Haircuts

Patient, friendly cuts for young gentlemen — many of our regulars started in this very chair.

Also available: haircut patterns & shape-ups, beard shaves and quick trims — just ask in the shop.
