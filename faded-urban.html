The Lounge

At Faded Urban Grooming Lounge we take pride in our work and provide all services to the best of our ability — holding a friendly atmosphere and keeping our environment professional. Inspired by American fashion, hip-hop trends and cultures worldwide, the lounge is a hub for the community.

While you wait there's a PlayStation, sports and music channels, and refreshments. Alongside barbering, the lounge also offers tattoos and merchandise — the one stop shop for all your urban needs.
