Questions & Answers

Everything you might want to know before you book.

How do I book an appointment?

Booking is by email — write to chrisbvb@hotmail.co.uk telling us what you would like and when suits you, and we will reply to confirm your appointment.

Do I need a skin test before colour?

Yes — a skin test is required at least 48 hours before any colour appointment. When you email to book colour, we will arrange your consultation and skin test first.

How much will my appointment cost?

Email for prices — send a message to chrisbvb@hotmail.co.uk with the service you are interested in and we will confirm the current price before you book. Colour is discussed at your consultation.

Where is the salon?

You will find us in Torquay. Email us at chrisbvb@hotmail.co.uk for directions and we will help you find us easily.

Do I need an appointment, or can I walk in?

We recommend booking by email so your appointment is reserved and completely unhurried. Send us a message first and we will find a time that suits you.

Can you do hair for a wedding or special occasion?

Yes — occasion hair and updos are one of our services. Email us with your event date and the look you have in mind, and we will plan your appointment together.
