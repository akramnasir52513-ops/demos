The Experience

The Full Turkish Experience

If you have never had a traditional Turkish barber service before, here is exactly what to expect when you take the chair at Naz.

A

The Consultation

Every appointment starts with a proper conversation. We look at your hair type, how it grows, and what you want from the cut — and we will be honest about what will work best for you. No guessing, no one-style-fits-all.

B

The Cut

Clippers and scissors in combination, worked section by section. Fades are blended gradually and checked in the mirror from every side as we go, so the gradient stays smooth and the shape stays balanced.

C

Hot Towels & Detail

Hot towels soften the skin and hair around the edges before the fine work: hairline, temples, around the ears and the neckline are cleaned up with a cut-throat razor for edges that look drawn-on sharp.

D

The Shave (If You Book It)

The hot towel shave is a ritual in itself — towels, rich lather, a steady single pass technique, then a cool towel to close everything down. It is as relaxing as it is precise, and the closest shave you can get.

E

The Finish

Loose hairs are brushed away, the cut is styled so you can see exactly how it sits, and we show you the back and sides in the mirror. Nothing leaves the chair until it looks right from every angle.

F

Keeping It Fresh

Before you go, we will tell you honestly how long the style should hold and when a tidy-up is worth it — so you can keep the same sharp look between visits without overdoing it.

Style Guide

Know What to Ask For

Not sure of the words for the cut you want? This quick guide covers the most requested styles at the shop — or just bring a photo and we will match it.

L

Low Fade

The fade starts just above the ears and curves around the head. Subtle, tidy and professional — a safe choice that suits almost everyone and grows out gracefully.

M

Mid Fade

Starts higher, around the temples, for a sharper contrast between the sides and the top. The most versatile fade — smart enough for work, sharp enough for the weekend.

H

High Fade

The boldest option: the fade climbs high and tight, leaving maximum contrast and a strong, clean outline. Best kept fresh with regular tidy-ups every couple of weeks.

T

Taper

Length is kept around the ears and neckline but blended shorter only at the very edges. A classic, natural finish for anyone who wants tidy without the skin-fade look.

S

Scissor Cut

All scissor work for a softer, more natural result with movement in the hair. Ideal for medium-length styles, grown-out looks and anyone avoiding a clippered finish.

C

Crop & Texture

Short on top with texture worked through it, usually worn forward with a blunt fringe. Low maintenance, modern, and works especially well with a mid or high fade.

1

Grooming Tip — Fade Timing

A skin fade looks sharpest in its first two weeks. Booking a tidy-up in between full cuts keeps the outline crisp without paying for a whole new cut every time.

2

Grooming Tip — Beard Lines

A beard always looks better with clean cheek and neck lines. If you trim at home, leave the shaping to us once a month — it resets the lines everything else follows.

3

Grooming Tip — Skin After Shaving

After any close shave, keep skin moisturised and avoid heavy fragranced products for a day. Hot towel shaves already finish with balm, which is why the skin feels calmer.

4

Grooming Tip — Growing It Out

Growing your hair longer does not mean skipping the barber. A light tidy every few weeks removes split, bulky ends while keeping the length — the shape stays intentional.

What is a Turkish hot towel shave?
It is the traditional way to shave: hot towels soften your beard and open the pores, a rich lather is applied, and a cut-throat razor gives an exceptionally close, clean result. A cool towel and balm finish it off, leaving skin smooth and calm.
Can you do skin fades?
Yes — low, mid and high skin fades are a speciality. Tell us how tight you want it and how much length you want left on top, and we will blend it seamlessly with a sharp, clean outline.
Do you cut children's hair?
Yes, younger customers are welcome. We take things at a calm pace so the experience is a positive one, from first haircuts through to school-ready styles and teenage fades.
Can I show you a photo of the style I want?
Absolutely — photos help a lot. Send it over on WhatsApp before your visit or bring it with you, and we will talk honestly about how it will work with your hair type and head shape.
How often should I get a fade touched up?
Most people find a fade looks its sharpest for around two weeks. A quick line-up and tidy between full cuts keeps the edges crisp and stretches the life of your cut.
What should I ask for if I want a change but nothing drastic?
Ask for your usual cut with a taper or a low fade on the sides — it tidies the outline and adds shape without changing your overall look. It is the safest first step if you have had the same style for years and want to freshen it up.
My hair is thinning — what style works best?
Shorter, textured styles usually work best: a crop or a tidy scissor cut with a soft taper keeps things looking fuller and neater. We will be honest about what suits your hair and will never sell you a style that will not hold.
How long does a full cut and beard service take?
It depends on the service and your hair, so message us when you book and we will give you a realistic idea. We would rather take the time to do it properly than rush you out of the chair.
Can I get just my beard done without a haircut?
Of course — beard trims and shapes are available on their own, with or without the hot towel treatment. Call or message for prices and we will confirm before you come in.
