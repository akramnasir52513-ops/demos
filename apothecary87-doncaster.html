About The Studio

Apothecary 87 is where old-school barbering meets modern grooming craft. At our Doncaster studio in the Yorkshire Hive, you can book a barber appointment or a salon appointment — proper cuts, proper care, and the Apothecary 87 product range behind every chair.

Free parking is available at the short stay car park. Walk in well, walk out better.
