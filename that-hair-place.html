About us

Two stylists, one much-loved local salon

That Hair Place was opened in 2015 by co-owners and senior stylists Leanne and Natalie — both qualified Wella technicians — and it has been looking after Heaton Moor's hair ever since.

The salon is proudly family friendly, with a dedicated children's area, complimentary refreshments while you relax, and up to two hours' free parking right outside on Shaw Road.

From first haircuts to full colour transformations, the team works with professional Wella, Olaplex and Osmo products — and 2,317 five-star-leaning Treatwell reviews suggest clients leave happy.
