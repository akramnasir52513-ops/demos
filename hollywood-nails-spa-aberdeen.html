How long will my nails last?

Gel typically lasts 2–3 weeks with care, while extensions usually need infills every 2–3 weeks as your nails grow. Avoid using your nails as tools and wear gloves for cleaning to help them last longer.

Do I need an appointment, or can I walk in?

We recommend booking by email so your time is reserved and you are not kept waiting. Email us to check the latest availability.

Can you remove gel or acrylic nails?

Yes, we offer removal services. Safe removal protects your natural nails, so please do not peel or force product off at home — email us to book a removal.
