The Shop

A Proper Local Barber

Jefferson Barber Shop sits on St Michael Street in the heart of Dumfries, in a traditional sandstone shopfront that locals know well. Inside, the focus is simple: a welcoming atmosphere, outstanding barbering and an affordable price.

The shop is always great with children, making haircuts a relaxed, stress-free experience for younger customers — and there is a complimentary beverage while you wait for your turn in the chair.

Mostly walk-ins, so there is no need to book ahead. Turn up, take a seat, and Jeff will fit you in.
