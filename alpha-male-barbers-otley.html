The Craft, Explained

What makes the Alpha finish

Fades, decoded

A skin fade is only as good as its blend. We work through the grades gradually, stretch the transition and keep checking the silhouette from the front, sides and back — because a fade has to look right from every angle, not just the barber's favourite one.

Not sure which fade suits you? High fades give a sharp, bold contrast; low fades keep it subtle and grown-up; a taper fade is the smart middle ground. Tell us the look you are after — or show us a photo — and we will match the blend to your head shape and hair growth.

The hot towel ritual

The hot towel is not a gimmick — it is the difference between a shave and an experience. Heat softens the beard hair and opens the pores, so the razor glides instead of dragging. The result is a closer finish with far less irritation, redness and razor burn.

Finished with a cold towel to close everything down and a proper tidy of the edges, it is the service gents book once out of curiosity — and then keep booking for good.

First visit? Here is how it goes

In the chair you can sit back and relax. When we are done we will show you the finish in the mirror from all sides, tidy up the neckline, and talk you through how to style it at home so it still looks sharp on day ten, not just day one.

Look After It

The Alpha grooming guide

A great cut deserves a little upkeep. Keep yours looking chair-fresh between visits with these basics — and ask us in the shop, we are always happy to talk you through your hair type.

Keeping a fade sharp

A skin fade grows out fastest at the edges, so most gents refresh every two to three weeks. Between visits:

  • Sleeping on a satin or smooth pillowcase reduces frizz on the blend
  • A light matte product keeps the top in place without shine
  • Do not attempt the neckline at home — one slip undoes the blend

Beard care basics

A shaped beard still needs looking after. A few minutes a week keeps the lines we carved looking deliberate:

  • Brush it daily to train the growth direction
  • A drop of beard oil after a shower keeps it soft, not wiry
  • Comb before you trim — never cut a tangled beard

Styling at home

The finish we give you in the chair is repeatable at home with the right routine:

  • Apply product to towel-dried, not soaking, hair
  • Work it from the back of the head forwards for even coverage
  • Less is more — you can always add, you cannot take away

Big occasion coming up?

Weddings, job interviews, photos, first dates — book The Full Works a day or two before the event, not the morning of. Your cut settles in, the skin calms after the shave, and you walk in looking effortlessly sharp rather than freshly clipped.
