Do I need to book, or can I just walk in?

Call us on 01202 747647 or email h73turkishbarber@gmail.com to arrange your visit — we will get you in the chair as soon as we can.

What is a traditional Turkish hot towel shave?

It is the classic Turkish barbering ritual: warm towels soften the beard, rich lather is worked in, and the shave is finished with a straight razor for an exceptionally close, comfortable result.

Do you cut children's and older gentlemen's hair too?

Yes — we cut all styles for men of all ages, from young lads to grandads.
