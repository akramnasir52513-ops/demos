Questions, answered

Do I need an appointment?

Walk-ins are welcome when we have space, but booking ahead is the safest way to get your preferred stylist and time.

What should I bring to a colour appointment?

Just yourself — and any photos of looks you love. We will talk through what suits your hair before we start.

Do you cut children's hair?

Yes — children are very welcome. Call us and we will find a time that suits the whole family.

Where can I park?

We are on Temple Street in central Aylesbury, close to town-centre car parks.
