Our Services

Classic barbering, done properly. Every service starts with a quick chat about what you want — and ends with a finish you can be proud of.

Haircuts

From a tidy short back and sides to a full restyle, your haircut is tailored to your head shape, hair type and how you wear it day to day. Consultation first, precision cutting throughout, and a styled finish so you walk out knowing exactly how it should look.

  • Consultation before the clippers come out
  • Cut to suit your hair type and lifestyle
  • Styled finish and honest styling advice

Skin Fades

A clean, sharp fade is all about the blend — and that's where we take our time. Whether you want a low, mid or high skin fade, every grade is blended seamlessly with no harsh lines, then finished around the edges for that crisp, fresh-out-the-chair look.

  • Low, mid or high skin fades
  • Seamless blending, no harsh lines
  • Edges tidied and finished

Beard Trims

Keep your beard looking deliberate, not accidental. We shape, tidy and define your beard to suit your face, clean up the cheek and neck lines, and finish so it sits sharp against your haircut — whether you keep it short and neat or long and full.

  • Shaped to suit your face
  • Cheek and neck lines cleaned up
  • Matched to your haircut for a complete look

Hot Towel Shaves

The traditional wet shave, done the way it should be. Hot towels open the pores and soften the hair, rich lather protects the skin, and a careful straight-razor shave leaves you smooth and refreshed — a proper bit of time out in the barber's chair.

  • Hot towel preparation
  • Traditional straight-razor shave
  • Cooling, soothing finish
