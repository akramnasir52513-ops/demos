Questions & Answers

Everything you might want to know before you book.

How do I book an appointment?

Bookings are taken via Nearcut — or email us at mancavelancaster@gmail.com, tell us what you would like and when suits you, and we will confirm your appointment.

Do I need a skin test before colour?

Yes — a skin test is arranged before any colour appointment. When you book colour, we will arrange your consultation and skin test first.

How much will my appointment cost?

Colour is priced by consultation, because every head of hair is different. For all other services, message for prices — email us and we will happily confirm.

Where is the salon?

We are in Lancaster. Email us for directions and to check the latest availability before your visit.

Can I book hair and beauty together?

Yes — hair and beauty are under one roof at Unit 8, so just tell us what you would like when you book and we will plan your visit.

I am not sure what I need — can I just ask?

Of course. Send us an email with a photo of your hair if you like, tell us what you are hoping for, and we will advise honestly — no obligation to book.
