Our Services

Each service is unhurried and precise, beginning with a consultation and ending with a finish worth admiring.

Haircuts

An elegant haircut is built on understanding — of your hair, your features and your daily routine. Your barber consults with you first, then cuts with a blend of scissor and clipper work for a shape that sits naturally and grows out gracefully. The appointment closes with styling and guidance for keeping the look at home.

  • Full consultation before cutting begins
  • Classic and contemporary styles alike
  • Styled finish with home-care guidance

Skin Fades

Our skin fades are defined by their transitions: gradual, flawless and tailored to the shape of your head. Whether low, mid or high, the blend is built patiently so there is never a visible line — only a smooth gradient into the skin, completed with refined, sharp edges.

  • Low, mid and high skin fades
  • Flawless, line-free transitions
  • Refined edges and neckline detail

Beard Trim & Hot Towel Shave

Beard work at Maguire’s is sculpture, not guesswork: the shape is balanced against your face, the lines are defined with a razor, and the hair is conditioned to a healthy finish. The hot towel shave remains the pinnacle — towels soften and prepare the skin for the closest, most comfortable shave.

  • Beard sculpted to your features
  • Hot towel preparation for closeness
  • Razor definition and conditioning finish

About Maguire’s Barbers

Crewe barbering, beautifully done

Maguire’s Barbers is a local Crewe barber shop where craftsmanship comes first. Our skilled barbers combine traditional techniques — hot towels, razor finishes, careful scissor work — with modern styles, so every client leaves with a look that feels both current and personal.

The shop is relaxed and walk-in friendly, yet every appointment receives the same unhurried attention. Whether it is a regular maintenance cut or a complete transformation, you will be listened to, advised honestly and looked after properly.

Questions & Answers

What you may wish to know before booking.

How do I book an appointment?

By email — write to maguiresbarberscrewe@gmail.com with the service you would like and your preferred time, and we will confirm your appointment promptly.

How much do services cost?

Please message for prices when you email to book — we will confirm the price for your chosen service in advance, clearly and without obligation.

Where is the shop located?

We are located in Crewe. Email us at maguiresbarberscrewe@gmail.com for directions and we will be delighted to guide you here.

Do I need an appointment, or can I walk in?

The shop is walk-in friendly, but an email booking secures your chair — particularly advisable at weekends and other busy periods.

I am unsure what to ask for — what should I do?

Simply tell us at your consultation. A photograph helps if you have one, but describing what you like is equally welcome — your barber will recommend a style tailored to your hair and features.

Are beard trims and shaves available?

Yes — beard trims and traditional hot towel shaves are among our signature services, available individually or within The Full Works.
