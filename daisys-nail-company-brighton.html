About Daisy's

Daisy's Nail Company & Spa sits on Preston Street in the heart of Brighton, offering vegan gel manicures, pedicures, eyelashes and massage in a calm, welcoming spa. With 500+ colours on the wall, there is a shade for every mood — from classic nude to full statement colour.

Every treatment is unhurried and careful, so you leave feeling polished, relaxed and properly looked after.
