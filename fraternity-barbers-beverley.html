Haircuts

From a tidy trim to a complete restyle, your haircut starts with a consultation about your hair type, your usual style and how you look after it at home. Your barber cuts with precision, checks every angle, and finishes the style so you leave knowing exactly how it will sit day to day.

  • Consultation before the clippers come out
  • Classic cuts and modern styles alike
  • Finished and styled before you leave

Skin Fades

A clean, sharp fade takes time and a steady hand — and that is exactly what you get here. Whether you prefer a low, mid or high fade, your barber blends it smoothly into the top, sharpens the edges, and checks the blend from every side so it grows out cleanly too.

  • Low, mid and high skin fades
  • Smooth, even blending with no harsh lines
  • Edges sharpened for a crisp finish

Beard Trims

A well-kept beard is shaped, not just shortened. Your barber tidies the length, defines the cheek and neck lines, and shapes your beard to suit your face — so it looks deliberate, clean and easy to maintain between visits.

  • Shape and length tailored to your face
  • Cheek and neck lines tidied and defined
  • Advice on keeping it sharp at home

Hot Towel Shaves

The traditional way to shave — and still the best. Warm towels soften the beard and open the pores, then a careful, close shave leaves your skin smooth and refreshed. It is as much a proper bit of time out as it is a grooming service.

  • Traditional hot towel preparation
  • Close, careful shave for a smooth finish
  • A relaxing, unhurried experience

What services do you offer?

We offer haircuts, skin fades, beard trims and hot towel shaves. If you are after something a little different, message us and ask — we are happy to advise.

Where are you based?

We are a barber shop in Beverley, in the East Riding of Yorkshire. You can also find us on Facebook, and book appointments through Booksy.

I'm not sure which style suits me — can you help?

Of course. Every appointment starts with a consultation, and you are welcome to bring a photo of a style you like. Your barber will advise honestly on what will work with your hair type and face shape.

What is a hot towel shave like?

Warm towels are used to soften your beard and prepare your skin, followed by a careful, close shave for a smooth, refreshed finish. It is a traditional treatment — relaxing as well as practical.
