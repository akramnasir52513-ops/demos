The Perfect Fade

What goes into a BarberZone fade? Here's the process, step by step.

1. The Consultation

Every fade starts with a quick chat: how high, how tight, and how you wear your hair day to day. We look at your head shape and growth patterns first, so the fade is planned — not guessed.

2. The Blend

Guard by guard, the fade is built up and blended until there's no visible line anywhere — just a smooth gradient from skin to length. We check it under the light and in the mirror from every angle.

3. The Finish

Razor-sharp edges around the hairline, neck and ears, then styled so you can see the finished look. We'll show you how to keep it looking fresh at home between visits.
