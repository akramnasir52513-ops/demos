Welcome to Harris & Fox

Since 2018, Harris & Fox has been a key player in Aberdeen's hair and beauty scene. Owner Ryan Harris wanted a light, bright space where guests can relax while having their hair vision come to life — and as an ex-builder, he designed the salon himself to make sure it's just perfect.

We're a collaborative workspace of experienced, creative freelance stylists, so every guest leaves feeling a million dollars. From ladies to gents, balayage to babylights, sharp short cuts to extensions — we've got you covered. Free consultations for all guests — let's chat!
