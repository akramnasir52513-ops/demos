The Craft

Why the details matter

Fades, blended by eye — not by numbers alone

A good fade isn't just running the guards up in order. We build the blend gradually, checking it in the mirror and in natural light, so there are no harsh lines and the fade grows out gracefully over the following weeks.

Not sure which fade suits you? Bring a photo, or just describe the look — we'll talk you through what will work with your hair and head shape before a single clipper touches your head.

A chair you're never rushed out of

We'd rather take an extra few minutes than send you out with a cut that's nearly right. Every appointment ends with a proper finish — neckline cleaned up, edges checked, and a look in the mirror from the back and sides so you leave confident.

And if something isn't sitting right a few days later, message us on WhatsApp and we'll sort it — that's how a local shop should work.

Traditional skills, modern standards

Hot towels, straight razors and scissor craft are the backbone of what we do — paired with clean tools, fresh blades and a tidy shop. It's the heritage Yorkshire barbering people remember, kept to the standard today's gents expect.

However you found us — a Google search, a recommendation, or wandering down Commercial Street — the welcome is the same.

Good to Know

Getting the most from your cut

How often should I get a fade?

Most gents keep a skin fade sharp with a tidy-up every two to three weeks. Classic scissor cuts hold their shape a little longer — around four to six weeks.

Keeping a beard in shape

Between visits, comb it through daily, keep the neckline where we set it, and don't trim the cheek lines higher than they were shaped — small tidy-ups only.

Not sure what to ask for?

Bring a photo of the style you like — it helps us match the length, blend and finish to your hair type. We'll always be honest about what will suit you.

New to the Shop?

Your first visit, step by step

Never been in before? Here's exactly what to expect when you take the chair at Harrogate Barbers.

1 · The chat

Every cut starts with a proper conversation. We'll ask what you're after, how your hair behaves, and how much time you spend styling it — so the cut fits your life, not just the mirror.

2 · The cut

Relax while we work through the cut or service you've chosen — clippers, scissors, razor work and hot towels where they're needed, with the blend and shape checked as we go.

3 · The finish & the check

We finish with styling, clean up the neckline and edges, and show you the back and sides in the mirror. Nothing leaves the chair until you're happy with it.

Before a big occasion

Weddings, interviews, photos and holidays all deserve a fresh cut. Book a few days ahead of the occasion itself so the cut has settled in — and tell us what it's for when you message.

Growing your hair out?

Growing out a cut doesn't mean skipping the barber. Regular tidy-ups keep the shape while the length comes through, so you never hit that awkward in-between stage.

Between-visit tidy-ups

If your fade just needs the edges freshening between full cuts, message us — we'll tell you honestly whether a quick tidy will do it or whether it's worth a full appointment.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Where is Harrogate Barbers?

We're at 4a Commercial Street, Harrogate, North Yorkshire — right in the town centre. If you're not sure where to find us, message us on WhatsApp and we'll point you in the right direction.

How do I book an appointment?

The quickest way is WhatsApp: message us on 07308 484046 with the service you'd like and when suits you. You can also call us on the same number, or use our Linktree and Facebook pages to get in touch.

How much does a haircut cost?

Prices vary by service, so we confirm the exact figure before we start — just call or message for prices. The price we quote is the price you pay, with no extras added at the till.

Do you take walk-ins?

When a chair is free, walk-ins are welcome — but booking ahead on WhatsApp is the safest way to guarantee your slot, especially at weekends and busy times.

What are your opening hours?

Hours can vary, so the best way to check today's availability is to message us on WhatsApp or give us a call — we'll let you know the chairs' availability straight away.

Do you do skin fades?

Yes — low, mid and high skin fades and tapers are among our most requested services. Tell us the finish you're after and we'll blend it to suit your head shape and hair type.

Do you offer hot towel shaves?

Yes, we offer a traditional hot towel shave: hot towel preparation, a careful straight-razor shave and a soothing finish. It's available on its own or as part of The Full Works.

Can you cut children's hair?

Of course — we offer patient, friendly kids' cuts. Booking ahead helps us allow a little extra time so younger gents never feel rushed.

What if I'm not sure what style I want?

Bring a photo, or just describe the look you're going for. We'll advise honestly on what will work with your hair, and agree the plan with you before we start.

Something not right with my cut?

Message us on WhatsApp and tell us — we'd always rather put it right. A quick tidy-up is part of looking after our customers properly.

How far ahead should I book?

For weekends and the run-up to holidays, a few days ahead is wise. Weekday chairs are often available sooner — message us and we'll tell you the next slot we have.

Do you cut long hair and classic styles?

Yes — not everything is a fade. Classic scissor cuts, longer styles and traditional gentleman's cuts are a big part of what we do. Just tell us the look you're keeping or growing towards.

Can I book for more than one person?

Of course — father-and-son cuts, mates before a night out, or a groom's party before a wedding. Mention how many chairs you need when you message us and we'll line the slots up together where we can.
